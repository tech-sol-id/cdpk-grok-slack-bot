1. Introduction
1.1. Company Profile
PT Citradata Purnakharisma has served as the official distributor of Motorola Solutions Indonesia since April 7, 1993. Citradata's vision is to become a trusted partner delivering exceptional experiences in communication solutions through Motorola Solutions technology, guided by our mission to:
1. Lead the communication solutions industry by providing high-quality products from Motorola Solutions that meet the highest industry standards.
2. Deliver superior service and technical support to comprehensively address customer requirements and ensure optimal system performance.
3. Foster robust long-term partnerships through continuous innovation and sustainable operational excellence that drives mutual growth and success.


1.2.         Report Objectives
This annual report aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of PT Citradata Purnakharisma's performance during fiscal year 2024, systematically identify operational challenges and market obstacles encountered, and articulate strategic development opportunities for future growth.
The report encompasses critical business dimensions including sales achievement analysis, marketing strategy effectiveness assessment, operational performance evaluation, human capital management review, and financial stability examination. This comprehensive analysis serves as the foundational framework for strategic planning initiatives designed to support sustainable organizational growth and enhanced market competitiveness in the evolving communications technology landscape.

2.1. Planning and Yearly Target


  

Gambar 1. Grafik Pertumbuhan Penjualan Tahunan


Based on the annual sales growth trajectory, the company has demonstrated consistent revenue expansion from 2019 through 2024. In 2019, revenue was recorded at Rp94,152,564,875, with modest growth to Rp94,680,014,563 in 2020. Significant acceleration occurred in 2021, with revenue reaching Rp135,714,266,771. The growth momentum continued in 2022, with sales advancing to Rp137,984,802,861.
The most substantial surge materialized in 2023, where revenue increased dramatically to Rp175,552,617,099. This exceptional performance was primarily driven by market supply constraints, which generated heightened demand for Motorola communication products, including Handy Talkies, Base Stations, and Repeaters. This market condition presented significant opportunities for the company to optimize its sales performance.
In 2024, revenue reached Rp180,787,154,457, representing a more moderate growth rate compared to the previous year, indicating market stabilization following the substantial surge in 2023. The 2024 revenue increase remains consistent with the progressive growth pattern established in preceding years. The positive trajectory from 2019 to 2024 demonstrates the company's successful capitalization of market opportunities, sustained sales enhancement, and maintenance of robust business performance throughout the period under review.

	Funnel marketing represents a strategic marketing model that illustrates the customer journey from initial awareness through to purchase conversion. This model is termed a "funnel" because the large volume of prospects at the beginning progressively narrows through selective processes at each stage, spanning awareness, consideration, and decision phases. Understanding each stage of this funnel enables companies to develop effective strategies for attracting, engaging, and converting prospects into customers.[1]
At the Top Funnel stage, strategy was executed through the "Organic Boost" campaign, designed to enhance engagement with relevant audiences. This campaign targeted Lookalike Audience (LA 1%) and Custom Audience (CA) utilizing the Instagram platform. The results demonstrated that the campaign successfully generated 91,518 organic interactions, including likes, comments, shares, and other forms of engagement. This figure indicates campaign success in capturing attention from new audiences with characteristics similar to existing audiences. Additionally, the "Audience Building" campaign focused on expanding reach and frequency (RnF) through Instagram and Facebook platforms. This campaign successfully reached 1,941,237 new audiences, demonstrating strategy effectiveness in expanding market potential and attracting relevant audience attention.
At the Mid Funnel stage, strategy concentrated on the "Instant Experience" campaign with objectives to increase reach and generate traffic to digital platforms. This campaign targeted all Custom Audiences (All CA), ensuring comprehensive coverage of relevant audiences. The campaign utilized Instagram (IG) and Facebook (FB) as primary channels, successfully achieving 1,170,397 reach during the campaign period. These results reflect strategic success in capturing audience attention and encouraging further content interaction, creating opportunities for audiences to progress to the next funnel stage.
At the Bottom Funnel stage, campaigns were executed through "Highlight/Promo" strategy specifically implemented during December. The campaign objectives were to increase engagement through Direct Message (DM) and WhatsApp (WA), while driving website traffic. This campaign targeted all Custom Audiences (All CA), utilizing Instagram (IG) and Facebook (FB) as primary channels. The results showed the campaign successfully generated 596 interactions, encompassing direct engagement and website visits. Although this figure was relatively smaller compared to previous stages, this was attributed to the limited campaign duration of December only. This strategy nevertheless provided significant contribution in directing audiences to direct communication platforms, strengthening audience relationships, and enhancing conversion opportunities.

	Keterbatasan keterampilan karyawan dapat menghambat efisiensi operasional.
	Keuangan
	Penurunan Laba Bersih: Penurunan laba bersih sebesar ±53,51% mengindikasikan tantangan dalam pengelolaan biaya operasional, pajak, atau faktor lainnya meskipun pendapatan meningkat.
	Daya saing menurun karena biaya tidak terkendali.
	Rasio Barang Masuk dan Keluar: Rasio 1,06 antara barang masuk dan keluar menunjukkan adanya potensi overstocking yang perlu diantisipasi untuk menghindari peningkatan biaya penyimpanan.
	Biaya penyimpanan meningkat dan memengaruhi arus kas.
	Barang Aging: Tingginya persediaan akhir sebesar 27.722 unit memerlukan strategi rotasi stok untuk menghindari barang usang.
	Berpotensi meningkatkan biaya penyimpanan dan risiko barang usang, yang dapat memengaruhi efisiensi operasional dan arus kas perusahaan.
	Berdasarkan tabel inventaris kendala dan dampak, perusahaan menghadapi berbagai tantangan yang memengaruhi operasional, pemasaran, sales, sumber daya manusia (SDM), dan keuangan. Dalam aspek operasional, keterlambatan pengiriman akibat kemacetan di Jabodetabek dan sulitnya akses ke daerah terpencil menjadi kendala yang menghambat efisiensi logistik. Selain itu, tingginya persediaan akhir sebesar 27.722 unit menimbulkan risiko barang aging yang dapat meningkatkan biaya penyimpanan dan memengaruhi efisiensi operasional. Meskipun jumlah barang rusak hanya mencapai 118 unit (0,43% dari sisa stok), hal ini tetap memerlukan perhatian untuk menjaga kualitas inventaris dan kepercayaan pelanggan.
Dalam pemasaran, kurangnya aktivitas promosi di wilayah potensial seperti Indonesia Timur mengakibatkan peluang pasar di wilayah tersebut belum tergarap secara maksimal. Selain itu, ketidakkonsistenan data pre-delivery pada semester pertama tahun ini menyulitkan perusahaan dalam menganalisis tren pemasaran secara menyeluruh, sehingga strategi yang lebih terarah sulit diterapkan.
Dari sisi sales, meskipun penjualan meningkat sebesar ±2,89%, penurunan laba bersih hingga ±53,51% menunjukkan adanya tantangan dalam pengelolaan efisiensi biaya dan operasional. Permintaan tinggi terhadap layanan after-sales seperti repair dan warranty juga membutuhkan sumber daya tambahan untuk menjaga kualitas layanan. Dalam aspek SDM, kebutuhan tenaga ahli dan pelatihan terfokus pada bidang teknis seperti instalasi, digital marketing, dan leadership menjadi prioritas untuk meningkatkan kapabilitas karyawan dalam menghadapi tantangan operasional dan ekspansi bisnis.
Pada aspek Strategic Partner dan proyek, kehilangan proyek besar seperti Telkom (TLT) dan Pertamina Hulu Rokan (PHR) memperjelas tantangan yang dihadapi, yaitu ketidakpuasan pelanggan terhadap kinerja produk tertentu dan kekalahan dalam tender kompetitif. Hal ini menggarisbawahi pentingnya peningkatan kualitas produk, terutama pada aspek teknis seperti kinerja baterai, serta strategi kompetitif yang lebih efektif untuk merebut proyek bernilai tinggi. Selain itu, kurangnya komunikasi yang mendalam dengan pelanggan pada tahap awal proyek juga menjadi penyebab utama kehilangan peluang.
Dari perspektif keuangan, rasio barang masuk terhadap barang keluar sebesar 1,06 menunjukkan potensi overstocking yang berisiko meningkatkan biaya penyimpanan dan memengaruhi arus kas. Penurunan laba bersih yang signifikan menjadi indikator perlunya pengendalian biaya lebih lanjut untuk menjaga daya saing perusahaan. Secara keseluruhan, tantangan yang dihadapi memengaruhi efisiensi, profitabilitas, dan daya saing perusahaan. Oleh karena itu, langkah strategis seperti optimalisasi logistik, pengelolaan stok yang lebih baik, peningkatan promosi, dan pengembangan kompetensi SDM sangat penting untuk memastikan keberlanjutan dan pertumbuhan bisnis yang lebih stabil.

8.         Penutup

Dengan tantangan seperti peningkatan persediaan akhir, kebutuhan tenaga ahli, serta tekanan pada layanan operasional, perusahaan tetap mampu menjaga stabilitas dan menunjukkan potensi besar untuk terus berkembang di masa mendatang.
Untuk mencapai target yang lebih tinggi di tahun depan, dibutuhkan komitmen kuat dari seluruh elemen perusahaan dalam mengatasi tantangan yang ada dan memanfaatkan peluang yang tersedia. Langkah strategis seperti optimalisasi proses operasional, diversifikasi produk, pengembangan SDM, dan peningkatan efisiensi pemasaran harus menjadi prioritas. Dengan kolaborasi yang solid, perusahaan dapat memastikan pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan dan daya saing yang semakin kuat di industri.
