The BARTEC SC9 EX1 Smartscanner is the world’s most compact  
5G smartscanner for Zone 0/1 and Div1. Featuring a integrated Zebra ®  
scan engine, it adds unparalleled scanning efficiency and accuracy.  
It shares a broad range of accessories, runs onAndroid™ 15, and offers 
comprehensive lifecycle support.  
The BARTEC SC9EX1 Smartscanner is crafted for professionals needing 
efficient scanning capabilities in hazardous environments. It is ideal for 
field workers, inventory managers, and safety inspectors who require 
precise and reliable data capture and communication in tough 
conditions. Its ability to record 4K video and capture high-quality still 
images in low light ensures comprehensive documentation and data 
accuracy, enhancing operational efficiency and safety.
