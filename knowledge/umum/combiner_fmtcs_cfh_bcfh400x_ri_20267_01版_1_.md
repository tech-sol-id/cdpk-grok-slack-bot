Introduction
The Combiner is designed specifically for two-way radio communication system(Repeater mode). It
features high reliability and excellent signal quality.
The Combiner adopting broadband coupling technology, it is available in multiple specifications and
is compatible with various carrier capacity systems.It also offers advantages including low insertion loss,
low voltage standing wave ratio (VSWR), dedicated port isolation design, as well as high power handling
and efficient heat dissipation capabilities.
