1 What is ISO/IEC 80079-34? 
 ISO/IEC 80079-34, entitled "Explosive Atmospheres — Part 34: Application 
of quality systems in the manufacture of equipment," specifies the 
requirements and guidelines for establishing and maintaining a quality 
management system to manufacture explosion-proof products that meet 
certification requirements. 
 
 It is built upon the foundation of ISO 9001, but incorporates numerous 
stringent "additional requirements" specifically tailored for explosion safety. 
 
 Core Objective: To ensure that every manufactured Ex product remains in 
full consistency with the type-tested sample (prototype) that was originally 
submitted to the laboratory for certification. 
 
 
2 Difference between ISO 80079-34 and ISO 9001  
While the two share a similar structure, ISO 80079-34 has nearly zero tolerance 
for risk. 
 ISO 9001: Focuses on customer satisfaction and continual process 
improvement. 
 ISO 80079-34: Focuses on compliance. Even if a customer is satisfied with 
the product, it constitutes a severe non-conformity if the product does not 
align with the explosion protection design specified in the certificate.
