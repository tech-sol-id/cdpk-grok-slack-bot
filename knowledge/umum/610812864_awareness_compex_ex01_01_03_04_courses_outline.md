INTRODUCTION:  
Course delegates will gain knowledge, understanding and practice in the preparation, installation, 
inspection and maintenance of equipment installed in potentially hazardous areas.  
Those people who should attend this course include electrical engineers, supervisors, and technicians 
who require awareness and understanding.  
The course also covers the standards for the manufacture and installation of equipment and their 
protection concepts where such equipment may be installed in potentially flammable atmospheres.  
Electrical installations in hazardous locations require a rigorous attention to detail. It is essential that 
equipment used in these locations be designed to the applicable s tandards and tested prior to 
installation to help ensure it does not initiate an explosion.  
This five days’ course will help you to understand requirements for designing, installing and maintaining 
electrical equipment in hazardous areas, providing a review of classifications based on division, zone, 
and dust, gas and fiber areas.  
You will examine installation  requirements based on applicable codes, standards and best practices. 
You will understand the differences between the Codes with respect to Division and Zone classifications, 
hazardous location product markings, and equipment construction & performance re quirements. You 
will also learn to evaluate prospective installations to determine appropriate wiring methods and 
equipment selection.

This course is required to get a better information make you able to path Comp “EX”01, 02, 03& 04
