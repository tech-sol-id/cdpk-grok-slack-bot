--- Halaman 12 ---
Directive 94/9/EC 
• 94/9/EC is the products directive concerning 
equipment and protective systems intended for 
use in potentially explosive atmospheres
• The directive is known as article 100a directive 
(referring to the treaty of Rome)
• Published in 1994 under the above number.
• It had a transitional period until 30 June 2003
• Applies to equipment put on the market or put in 
service after 30 June 2003
• It is mandatory superseding national standards.

--- Halaman 13 ---
ATEX Directive 94/9/EC 
Key Elements
• Introduces the idea of equipment 
categories.
• Design and manufacturing of electrical and 
non-electrical equipment - certification etc.
• Mixture of flammable substances in the 
form of gases, vapours, mists and dusts 
with air under atmospheric conditions
• Ignition sources
• New Equipment Grouping Terminology
• New Labelling Standards

--- Halaman 14 ---
ATEX Directive 94/9/EC 
Main Objectives
• Guarantee free circulation of goods in the European 
Union by aligning the technical and legal 
requirements in the member states
• Ensure high level of safety and protection against 
explosion
• Replace existing divergent National and European 
legislation covering the same subject

--- Halaman 19 ---
Directive 1999/92/EC
Main Objectives 
• To establish the minimum requirements to protect and 
improve the Safety and Health of Workers at risk from 
explosive atmospheres.
• Identify the hazards and assess the risks
• Lay down specific measures to safe guard the safety and 
health of workers at risk from explosive atmospheres.
• To ensure a safe working environment and appropriate 
monitoring during the presence of workers in accordance 
with the risk assessment.
• To take necessary steps and make the necessary 
arrangements for the coordination when several 
firms/contractors are working in the same place.
• To produce an explosion protection document (EPD).
• Both directives are complementary.

--- Halaman 20 ---
Directive 1999/92/EC
Assessment of Explosion Risks
In order to achieve the main objectives of directive 
1999/92/EC the assessment should at least take 
account of:
• The likelihood that explosive atmospheres will occur 
and their persistence.
• The likelihood that ignition sources will be present 
and become active and effective
• The installation, substances used, processes, and 
their possible interactions
• The scale of anticipated effects.
• Explosion risks should be assessed overall .

--- Halaman 21 ---
Risk Assessment
A risk assessment must be carried out for every 
work or production process and for every 
operational status and change of status of a plant. 
It should be based on the following operational 
statuses:
1. Normal operating conditions, including 
maintenance.
2. Commissioning and decommissioning.
3. Malfunctions and foreseeable faults.
4. Misuse which can be reasonably foreseen.

--- Halaman 22 ---
Organisational Measures
The employer is required to take technical and / or 
organisational measures including: 
1. Produce written instructions, 
2. Training of workers in explosion protection
3. Ensure workers have adequate protection
4. Apply a permit to work system
5. Carry out maintenance
6. Carry out inspection and monitoring
7. Mark hazardous places
8. The measures shall where necessary be combined and / 
or supplemented with measures preventing the 
propagation of explosions and shall be reviewed 
regularly, and in any event whenever significant changes 
occur.

Safety of installation
Annex II of the Directive

use in explosive atmospheres, should have 
“sufficient and appropriate training” to 
complete the work in a safe and competant

--- Halaman 26 ---
Explosion Protection Document
The Employer shall draw up and maintain up to date 
a document demonstrating that:
– the explosion risks have been determined and assessed,
– that adequate measures will be taken to attain the aims of the 
Directive,
– hazardous areas are classified in accordance with Annex I of the 
directive,
– places where minimum requirement set out in ANNEX II of the 
directive will apply are identified,
– that the workplace and work equipment are safe,
– arrangements have been made for the safe use of equipment.
The document must be drawn up prior commencement of work
and be revised when the workplace, work equipment or 
organisation 
of the work undergoes significant changes, extensions or 
conversions.

--- Halaman 27 ---
Explosion Protection Document 
(EPD)
• EPD is mandatory.
• No pre-defined format.
• Must be prepared before commencement of 
work and be revised as necessary.
• Explosion risk assessment is mandatory.
• Existing documents may be used to form 
part of the existing protection document.
• Can be combined with documentation 
produced under other EU requirements.
