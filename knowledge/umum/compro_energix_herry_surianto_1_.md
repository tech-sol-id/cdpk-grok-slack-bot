About Us
Bluetti
Poweroak Technology Co. Ltd. was established in 2013, in
Shenzhen China. In 2019 Poweroak Technology released
products under the brand BLUETTI.
Poweroak Technology Co., Ltd. is a manufacturer or
principle company for energy storage products such as
Portable Power Stations, Home Battery Backup, Solar
Generator and other Accessories to meet the increasing
demand for renewable energy by consumers.
Energix
PT Energix Power Indonesia is the Master Distributor for
BLUETTI in Indonesia since November 2023.
ENERGIX is committed to providing sustainable and
high-quality energy solutions. A variety of innovative
products such as portable power stations, solar panels,
and battery home backup systems are designed and
customized to meet diverse needs across communities
and industries.
ENERGIX already has a network of authorized
distributors and dealers spread across Greater Jakarta,
Bali, NTB, NTT, Lombok, West Java, Central Java, East
Java, Sumatra, Banjarmasin, Pekanbaru, Riau & Papua.

Product
Overview
Portable Power Station
Bluetti Portal Power Station is a reliable battery-powered 
storage system coming in different sizes and power capacities 
to meet your needs.
Energy Storage and Backup System
In case of emergencies and unexpected blackouts, BLUETTI’s Home 
Backup Series provides abundant power for your appliances, 
reducing your reliance on the grid while saving your electricity bills 
in to the long run.
Expandable Battery Series
BLUETTI expansion battery is a portable external battery system 
that can serve as a power source itself, or reliable energy boost
when connected to compatible solar power generator, provide you 
with a larger capacity to power devices for extended runtime.
Portable Solar Panel
In case of emergencies and unexpected blackouts, BLUETTI 
portable solar panels, also known as photovoltaics (PV), convert 
sunlight into electricity to power your devices anytime.
