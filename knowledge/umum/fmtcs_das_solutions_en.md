--- Slide 8 ---
Limitations of Traditional Two-Way Radio Systems
Signal Interference
Co-channel and adjacent-channel interference degrades voice quality and causes dropped calls.
Dead Zones
Basements, elevators, and steel structures block RF signals creating areas with no communication.
Limited Transmission Distance
Coaxial cable attenuation limits coverage to a few hundred meters from the base station.
Difficult Troubleshooting
Fault isolation requires manual inspection of each cable segment, causing extended downtime.
High Maintenance Costs
Frequent cable inspections, connector replacements, and amplifier adjustments drive up OPEX.
No Expandability
Adding new coverage areas requires replacing coaxial runs, disrupting existing infrastructure.
No Centralized Monitoring
No visibility into system health; problems are only detected when users report communication failures.
Result: Poor reliability, high operational costs, and communication blind spots
