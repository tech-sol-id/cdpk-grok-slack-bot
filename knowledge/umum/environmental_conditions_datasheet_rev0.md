1. INTRODUCTION 
1.1 Project Background 
The Abadi Field is located in the Masela Block in the Arafura Sea, Maluku Province, which is 
located more than 12 miles from the nearest island. The Masela Block is located approximately 
750 km south of Ambon, the capital city of Maluku Province. Water depth in the field ran ges 
from 400 m to 800 m. The southern boundary of the block is adjacent to the agreed Indonesia-
Australia maritime border. The entire block is located in Indonesia side (no cross border). 
 
 
Figure 1-1 Abadi Field Location 
There are no existing production facilities in the proximity of the Abadi Field, therefore the 
development plan for the Abadi Field is planned to consist of subsea facilities (including 
subsea wellheads, drilling centers, Subsea Umbilicals Risers and Flowli nes (SURF), CO2 
injection well, and SURF for CCS facility), Floating Production Storage and Offloading (FPSO) 
facility, Gas Export Pipeline (GEP) and CO2 pipeline, and an Onshore LNG (OLNG) plant 
including Onshore CCS facility located at Yamdena Island in Kepulauan Tanimbar Regency 
(KKT).  
The FPSO contains 2 -train gas processing facility to export dry gas to the LNG processing 
facility in onshore (OLNG) through the GEP where the throughput production in OLNG is 9.5 
MTPA consists of 2 trains of 4.75 MTPA and 150 MMSCFD gas pipe for other industries built 
by others.  
The CO2 from the Acid Gas Removal Unit (AGRU) of LNG trains is dehydrated and 
compressed. The CO2 is sent to a subsea injection well at Abadi field through the CCS pipeline 
installed parallel to the GEP.

3. PURPOSE  
The purpose of this document is to define the basis for the environmental conditions data 
sheets which shall be used for the requisition of equipment located on the FPSO.    
The relevant environmental conditions data sheets shall be included in each equipment 
requisition to provide the necessary information to equipment suppliers.  It is the responsibility 
of the requisitioning discipline to identify the relevant data sheet or  data sheets that are 
applicable to their equipment, which shall be based on the location of the equipment.  
This document defines the FPSO specific metocean design criteria for the Abadi Field. All data 
are extracted directly from the Basis Of Design - Metocean at Abadi Field Location (In-Field) 
(O2190-MO-BOD-00001, Rev.08).

4.5.3 Tsunami 
Tsunamis in this region can occur as low-amplitude waves, typically less than 1 meter in height 
with extremely long wavelengths spanning hundreds of kilometers. These waves travel at high 
speeds, ranging from 500 to 800 km/h. As a result, the potential impact on offshore 
developments in deep water is considered to be low. 
While there have been industry reports of tsunami waves affecting vessels moored side -by-
side, such incidents are rare. To mitigate these risks, the BOM Tsunami Warning Service will 
be actively monitored, and appropriate safety measures will be implemented should a tsunami 
threat be identified.

Criteria for sea level raise and temperature 
Sea level rise change affects the strength design for mooring and riser hence it is a critical 
factor for integrity while temperature mainly affects performance of process and mechanical 
equipment. Also, from middle of 2040s the gas production (POD basis) will start declining 
hence 100% performance may not be required. Therefore, the criteria for sea level raise is 
more stringent than that of temperature. 
Note that RCP (Representative Concentration Pathway) 6.0 scenario in IPCC AR5 (5 th 
Assessment Report) had been used to assess climate change impact on sea level rise,  air 
temperature and seawater temperature following the IOGP recommendation  
(https://www.iogp.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Workshop-Exec-Summary.pdf) prior to 
IPCC AR6 (6th Assessment Report) issuance. We have compared the RCP scenarios in AR5 
and SSP scenarios in AR6 and SSP2 -4.5 in AR6 is now selected for  further assessment of 
climate change impact on sea level rise, air temperature and  seawater temperature
