SURAT PENGANGKATAN

EX AUTHORIZED PERSON PERLINDUNGAN EKSPLOSIF

(Letter of Appointment for Authorized Person of Explosion Protection)

Dalam rangka memenuhi persyaratan standar ISO/IEC 80079-34:2018 tentang sistem manajemen mutu untuk produk explosion-proof (Ex), PT Citradata Purnakharisma dengan ini menunjuk:

sebagai Ex Authorized Person (ExAP) PT Citradata Purnakharisma yang bertanggung jawab atas manajemen dan pengawasan persyaratan teknis serta kepatuhan produk explosion-protected yang ditangani perusahaan, memastikan seluruh produk memenuhi regulasi dan persyaratan sertifikasi perlindungan eksplosif yang berlaku.

TANGGUNG JAWAB MELIPUTI:

1. Manajemen Teknis Produk Ex (i-safe Mobile & The Joy Factory):

Memastikan kegiatan service, repair, dan instalasi produk Ex sesuai dengan ISO/IEC 80079-34, IEC 60079-19, dan standar teknis yang berlaku; memastikan implementasi ketat dalam proses workshop.

2. Pengawasan Kepatuhan & Regulasi:

Mengawasi proses workshop untuk memastikan kepatuhan terhadap semua regulasi dan persyaratan sertifikasi yang berlaku dari i-safe Mobile GmbH dan The Joy Factory.

3. Manajemen Dokumen & Rekaman Ex:

Mereview dan menyetujui semua dokumen yang berkaitan dengan produk Ex sebelum controlled release; memastikan rekaman Ex dikelola dengan akurasi, kelengkapan, dan keterlacakan (minimum 10 tahun sesuai ISO/IEC 80079-34:2018 Klausul 7.5.3).

4. Manajemen QAR/PQAN dari Manufacturer:

Menerima, meninjau, dan mendistribusikan Quality Assessment Report (QAR) dan Product Quality Assurance Notice (PQAN) tahunan dari i-safe Mobile GmbH dan The Joy Factory; memastikan pengelolaan sebagai external document sesuai prosedur yang berlaku.

5. Manajemen Training Ex:

Memantau dan memastikan kelengkapan serta efektivitas training Ex untuk semua Personnel Influencing Ex Product Conformity (PIEPC) di PT Citradata Purnakharisma; mengikuti minimal satu external training Ex per tahun.

6. Komunikasi Eksternal & Koordinasi:

Bertindak sebagai representatif utama PT Citradata Purnakharisma dalam komunikasi dengan i-safe Mobile GmbH, The Joy Factory, dan Certification Body terkait segala hal yang berkaitan dengan sertifikasi Ex dan compliance requirements.

7. Notifikasi Perubahan:

Bertanggung jawab untuk menghubungi i-safe Mobile GmbH dan The Joy Factory apabila terjadi pergantian ExAP, dalam waktu 14 hari kerja setelah pengangkatan resmi.

Surat Pengangkatan ini berlaku sejak tanggal penandatanganan dan dapat diperbarui berdasarkan keputusan manajemen atau perubahan standar yang berlaku.

LETTER OF APPOINTMENT

AUTHORIZED PERSON FOR EXPLOSION PROTECTION

(Surat Pengangkatan Ex Authorized Person — PT Citradata Purnakharisma)

In accordance with the requirements of ISO/IEC 80079-34:2018 (Quality Management Systems for Ex Products), PT Citradata Purnakharisma hereby formally appoints:

as the Authorized Person for Explosion Protection of PT Citradata Purnakharisma, responsible for managing and overseeing the technical requirements and compliance of the company's explosion-protected products (i-safe Mobile devices and The Joy Factory ATEX cases), ensuring that all products meet the relevant explosion protection regulations and certification requirements.

RESPONSIBILITIES INCLUDE:

1. Technical Management of Ex Products (i-safe Mobile & The Joy Factory):

Ensure that all service, repair, and installation activities for Ex products comply with ISO/IEC 80079-34, IEC 60079-19, and applicable technical standards; enforce strict implementation within the workshop process.

2. Compliance & Regulatory Oversight:

Monitor the workshop process to ensure compliance with all applicable regulations and certification requirements of i-safe Mobile GmbH and The Joy Factory.

3. Ex Document & Records Management:

Review and approve all documents related to Ex products prior to controlled release; ensure Ex records are managed with accuracy, completeness, and traceability (minimum 10 years in accordance with ISO/IEC 80079-34:2018 Clause 7.5.3).

4. QAR/PQAN Management from Manufacturers:

Receive, review, and distribute the annual Quality Assessment Report (QAR) and Product Quality Assurance Notice (PQAN) from i-safe Mobile GmbH and The Joy Factory; ensure management as external controlled documents in accordance with applicable procedures.

5. Ex Training Management:

Monitor and ensure the completeness and effectiveness of Ex training for all Personnel Influencing Ex Product Conformity (PIEPC) at PT Citradata Purnakharisma; attend at least one external Ex training per year.

6. External Communication & Coordination:

Act as the primary representative of PT Citradata Purnakharisma in communications with i-safe Mobile GmbH, The Joy Factory, and the Certification Body regarding all matters related to Ex certification and compliance requirements.

7. Change Notification:

Responsible for contacting i-safe Mobile GmbH and The Joy Factory in the event of an ExAP change, within 14 working days of the official appointment.

This Letter of Appointment is effective from the date of signing and may be renewed based on management decisions or changes in applicable standards.
