Letter of Appointment for Authorized Person

of Explosion Protection

In accordance with the requirements of the ISO 80079-34 standard, our company hereby appoints            as the authorized person for explosion protection.

He will be responsible for managing and overseeing the technical requirements and compliance of the company’s explosion-protected products, ensuring that all products meet the relevant explosion protection regulations and certification requirements.

Responsibilities include:

Technical Management of Explosion-Protected Products: Ensure that the design and manufacturing of explosion-protected products comply with ISO 80079-34 and other relevant technical standards, and ensure strict implementation during the production process.

Compliance and Regulatory Supervision: Oversee the production process of explosion-protected products to ensure compliance with all applicable regulations and certification requirements.

Documentation and Record Management: Manage all technical documents related to explosion-protected products, ensuring their accuracy, completeness, and traceability.

External Communication and Coordination: Act as the company representative responsible for communicating with external organizations(such as, certification bodies,clients,etc.) to ensure that explosion protection products meet certification and compliance requirements.
