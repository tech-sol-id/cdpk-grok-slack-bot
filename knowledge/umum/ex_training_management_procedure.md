Purpose

To ensure that all personnel whose activities affect the conformity of Ex products (including Sales, Procurement, R&D, HR, Quality Assurance, Marketing, etc.) have received appropriate training and possess adequate knowledge and competence.

To ensure that personnel directly involved in activities related to Ex product quality, such as manufacturing, assembly, and inspection, have undergone specific job-skills training.

Scope

This procedure applies to all personnel influencing Ex product conformity, including Ex Authorized Persons, and staff involved in manufacturing, inspection, testing, sales, marketing, supply chain management, calibration, and quality management services.

Definitions

Internal Training (In-house): Tailor-made training programs centered on core organizational needs to enhance the professional skills and knowledge of personnel..

External Training: Individual participation by personnel in public courses organized by professional Ex organizations.

Qualification of Ex Authorized Person: Must have completed training for IECEx 05 Unit Ex 000 and Ex 001, and hold a valid certificate of completion.

IECEx 05 : IECEx Scheme for Certification of Personnel Competence for Explosive Atmospheres. 

IECEx 05 Unit Ex 000 : Basic knowledge and awareness to enter a site which includes a classified hazardous area.

IECEx 05 Unit Ex 001: Apply basic principles of protection in explosive atmospheres.

Responsibilities and Authorities

General Manager / CEO: Approval of budgets and expenses for Ex-related education and training.

Ex Authorized Person

Continuously monitor and ensure the completeness and effectiveness of training within the Ex Quality Management System (ExQMS).

Participate in at least one external training session annually, such as Ex seminars, workshops, or professional certification courses.

Periodically review the training plans to ensure that all "Personnel Influencing Ex Product Conformity" have completed mandatory Ex Basic Knowledge training.

Human Resources & Administration Dept.

Responsible for the management and retention of Ex training records, assessment results, and personnel certificates.

Responsible for the integration, planning, and implementation of internal Ex training programs across all departments.

Responsible for selecting external Ex training providers and coordinating logistics for training activities.

TJF shall appoint qualified professionals to be responsible for conducting Standard Installation Training for Ex products.

Trainees must undergo a Practical Assessment. The training records and assessment results shall be documented in the "Ex Internal Training Master List."

Quality records related to Ex product conformity must be retained for a minimum of 10 years.
