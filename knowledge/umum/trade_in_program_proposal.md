TRADE IN PROGRAM PROPOSAL DRAFT


On behalf of PT Citradata Purnakharisma, the authorized distributor of Motorola Solutions in Indonesia, we would like to propose the implementation of a Trade-In Program aimed to accelerate product revitalization and strengthen customer retention across key market segments.
Program Objective
The main objective of this Trade-In Program is to encourage existing users of legacy or non-Motorola communication devices to upgrade to the latest Motorola Solutions products, by offering them attractive trade-in value for their old units. This initiative will:
* Promote migration to advanced digital technologies.


* Increase sales volume for new models, the MOTOTRBO series.


* Reinforce brand loyalty among current and potential users.


* Compete with other brands, especially Hytera and other China brands. 

Program Mechanism (Proposed)
   * Eligibility: Customers owning old or outdated communication devices (Motorola or other brands).

   * Valuation: Trade-in value determined based on the brand's value. All kinds of conditions will be valued at the same rate.

   * Purchase Incentive: Trade-in value deducted directly from the purchase price of eligible new Motorola Solutions products.

   * Execution: Citradata will coordinate the collection and verification of old units, supported by Motorola’s approval and promotional materials.

   * Duration: Suggested program period of 3 months in Q1 2026, extendable upon review of performance and market response.

Targeted Market
Based on our nationwide survey, the majority of users remain highly satisfied with their existing Motorola devices, particularly the GP and CP series. These models are well-regarded for their durability and reliability, with many units still operating effectively after more than 20 years. However, users now expect the same level of toughness—if not improved performance—from the latest MOTOTRBO series.
Most of these respondents are from the hospitality and tourism sectors, segments where Motorola Solutions Indonesia has faced challenges in both reach and retention. To strengthen our position, it is crucial to listen closely to their feedback and align with their operational needs. We have carefully compiled these insights to encourage Motorola Solutions to take this important initiative—starting with a small yet strategic step that can lead to significant growth within Indonesia’s untapped hospitality and tourism markets.
