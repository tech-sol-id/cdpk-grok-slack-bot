Content & Process

The documentation shall cover the following management instructions:

Management of External Documents: Define the control procedures for "External Documents," including but not limited to: methods for storage upon receipt, protocols for ensuring correct usage, and procedures for the collection and disposal of obsolete documents.

Identification and Retention of Records: Since the retention period for records related to Ex products is a minimum of 10 years, the management and identification of Ex-related records versus non-Ex records must be clearly defined. This ensures that all records pertinent to explosion protection are properly controlled and retained.

Ex-related Records shall include: ( Reference: ISO/IEC 80079-34:2018, 7.5.3 (j))

those arising from regulatory requirements;

quality documented information;

responsibilities and authorities for Ex relevant roles assignment and communication

within the organization;

customer order;

contract review;

training records;

design and development changes;

inspection and test data (per batch);

calibration data;

manufacturing traceability;

sub-contractor evaluation;

delivery data (customer, delivery date and quantity, including serial numbers where

available);

other documented information, if needed.
