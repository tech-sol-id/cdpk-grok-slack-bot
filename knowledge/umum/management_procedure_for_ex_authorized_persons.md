Purpose

To ensure the Top Management appoints Ex Authorized Persons (Responsible Persons) for the Ex Quality Management System (ExQMS), announces these appointments company-wide, and defines their responsibilities and authorities.

To define the change management process when there is a turnover of Ex Authorized Persons.

Scope

This procedure applies to the appointment and management of Authorized Persons within the company’s Ex Quality Management System (ExQMS).

Definitions

Ex Authorized Person (Responsible Person): An individual (or individuals) appointed by Top Management to oversee tasks related to Ex product certification, including regulatory liaison and internal coordination.

Responsibilities and Authorities

General Manager / CEO: Responsible for appointing Ex Authorized Persons and defining their scope of responsibility.

HR & Administration Dept.:

Responsible for the official announcement of appointments.

Responsible for maintaining the registry of Ex Authorized Persons and Factory Ex Authorized Persons.

Factory Ex Authorized Person 

Periodically attend external Ex-related training, such as seminars and workshops, on an annual basis.

Possess a clear understanding of Ex product manufacturing processes and quality control operations.

Act as the primary contact point for the factory to effectively coordinate Ex product manufacturing processes and quality control activities.

Content & Process

Appointment of Ex Authorized Person

The CEO appoints the Ex Authorized Person. HR issues the Letter of Appointment (Ex-QR0532-02). QMD registers the information in the Ex Authorized Person Management List (Ex-QR0532-01)

Management of Change

Appointment and Announcement: When replacing an Ex Authorized Person, the General Manager (CEO) shall make the appointment , followed by a formal announcement issued by the HR & Administration Department.

Handover Process: If necessary, the outgoing Ex Authorized Person must submit a "Handover Checklist". This ensures that the newly appointed successor can promptly and accurately follow up on all outstanding tasks and ongoing operations.

Notification to Manufacturers: The newly appointed Ex Authorized Person is responsible for contacting the Manufacturers listed on the QAR/PQAN to notify them of the change in authorized personnel.

Records Management: The change information must be reported to the Document Control Center (DCC) to update the "Ex Authorized Person Management List" (Ex-QR0532-01) for ongoing record management.

The QAR/PQAN product manufacturer periodically provides the verification results of standards related to the Ex Certificates on an annual basis; upon receipt, these verification documents shall be managed in accordance with external document control procedures.

Various production documents, quality control documents, and operational forms required for the Ex product assembly process must be reviewed and approved by the Ex Authorized Person prior to controlled release, ensuring that the Ex products maintain full conformity with the Ex Certificate.

Quality records related to Ex product conformity must be retained for a minimum of 10 years.
