2 Testing 
A basic check is recommended. 
 
1. Make detailed visual inspection 
(all sides). 
2. Test charging by desktop charger, USB C and Magnetic jack with the i.safe protectors from the set. 
3. Insert SIM and see if unit will register in network. 
4. Make a call to 2nd person end evaluate sound quality in both directions. 
 
If special testing needed, please check the basic functions, like touch, LCD, speaker, mic, receiver, … 
 
 
3 Maximizing Battery Lifespan      
 
1. Do not fully discharge your device’s battery - the battery could fall into a deep discharge state, which 
renders it incapable of holding a charge. 
2. Power down the device to avoid additional battery use e.g. when charging. 
3. If you plan to store your device for longer than 3 months, charge it 50 percent every 3 months. If you 
store it fully charged for an extended period, the battery may lose some capacity, leading to shorter 
battery life. 
 
 
Best practices 
 
1. It is better to charge with USB C – magnetic charging takes longer to power your phone. 
2. Do not charge the device for over 2 nights, just until the device is 100% green LED. 
3. For optimized battery life, your phone should never go below 20 percent. 
 
NOTE: Only charge the device using the i.safe Cable or other charging equipment approved by i.safe 
MOBILE.
