ORU（Fiber Optical Remote Unit）
Professional device for indoor radio signal coverage
Introduction
The ORU is designed for two-way radio systems. The ORU adopts photoelectric conversion
technology.It can providing a high-quality signal boost for concur-rent communication and longer
transmission coverage.
Information
Name: ORU
Model: FMTCS-LRF/F
Spec.code AORU400CH.RI1
Description
The ORU receive and acquire the optical signal transmitted by the OMU.And demodulates the optical
signal back into RF signal, and amplifies it.
Meanwhile, the ORU will process the walkie-talkie signal in the same way and transmit it to the OMU
according to the same principle.
