AUTHORIZED PARTNER AGREEMENT

Date: 5th February, 2026

ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN:

PARTY A,

Shenzhen Chainway Information Technology Co., Ltd. [9/F Building 2, Phase 2, Gaoxinqi Industrial Park, liuxian 1st Rd., District 67, Bao’an, Shenzhen, Guangdong 518102, China], hereafter referred to as the “Chainway ”.

AND PARTY B,

     [partner name and address], hereafter referred to as the “Partner”.

WHEREAS:

Chainway, a professional provider of products and solutions of RFID, barcode and biometrics technologies, owns the trademark Chainway (the « Trademark »). Chainway markets Mobile Computers, RFID Readers, Rugged Tablets, Biometric Readers, RFID Modules, Printers, and Vehicle-mounted Computers under the Trademark (the “Products”).

The Partner is private company under Indonesian law, named PT Teknologi Chayani Harsa, a leading company for the supply of products and services for AIDC. It is recognized in its business field.

The Parties wish to establish a cooperation to promote the integration and application of Chainway's Products within Partner's solutions and projects for end clients.

Discussions took place and the Parties decided to enter into this agreement, which, including its recitals and its appendices that incorporate into it and are indivisible, is hereafter referred to as the “Agreement” or the ”Contract ”. NOW THEREFORE, THE PARTIES HEREBY AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

1 – NATURE OF COOPERATION

Chainway and the Partner hereby agree to establish a non-exclusive partnership. This Agreement sets forth the framework for the Partner to preferentially recommend, integrate, and use Chainway's Products within the solutions, system integration, or service projects it provides to its end clients. Chainway agrees to provide the Partner with necessary technical, pricing, and marketing support to facilitate this cooperation.

2 – FRAMEWORK OF COOPERATION

2.1 – MODE OF COOPERATION

The Partner shall integrate Chainway's Products into its own solutions or projects and directly contract with, invoice, and assume primary responsibility for the project towards the end client. The Partner shall purchase the Products from Chainway as required for the fulfillment of these projects.

2.2 – TERRITORY

The primary focus of this cooperation shall be within [Indonesia] (the "Territory"). The Parties may jointly pursue opportunities outside the Territory on a case-by-case basis.

2.3 – MARKETING AND PROMOTION

2.3.1 Chainway's Responsibilities

Chainway shall invest not less than 1 million USD into digital marketing & international exhibitions, including labor, design, websites, promotional materials, videos, B2B, SEO, exhibitions, on-site support which shall be considered in Chainway’s Global markets.

Chainway will provide the Partner, free of charge, with content, images, and texts relating to its Products. The Parties may, on a case-by-case basis, jointly plan and execute co-marketing activities. The scope, responsibilities, and cost-sharing for any such activity shall be mutually agreed upon in writing prior to its commencement.

2.3.2 Partner’s Responsibilities

The Partner shall bear expenses for advertising and publicity within the aforementioned territory in the duration of this Agreement and submit to Chainway all patterns and/or drawings and descriptions for prior approval.

The Partner acknowledges that any action, promotion, canvassing or communication relating to Chainway or to the Products shall respect their image and their positioning and shall comply with Chainway’s quality standards as well as with its marketing policy. The Partner will comply with all the obligations applying to his activities and will in particular ensure that the distribution of the Products and his promotion actions are always perfectly lawful and appropriate considering the aim pursued, in order to preserve the reputation and the image of Chainway and of the Products.

2.4 – INTERNET

The Partner must have its official commercial website where Chainway products can be displayed, the Partner shall prepare fully localized promotional materials including printing brochures, catalogues, posters, and electronic datasheets.

The Partner shall obtain the authorization of Chainway as to the plan and the content of the Pages, prior to their initial publishing, their modification, or update.

The Partner shall bear all the costs related to these Pages, notably development, hosting, referencing, search engine optimization, and exploitation.

2.5 – SALES REPORTS

In order to keep Chainway well informed of the prevailing market conditions, the Partner should undertake to supply Chainway, at least once a quarter or at any time when necessary, with market reports concerning changes of the local regulations in connection with the import and sales of the commodity covered by this agreement, local market tendency and the Partner's comments on quality, packing, price, etc. of the goods supplied by Chainway under this agreement. 

2.6 – SECURITY, CONFORMITY, GUARANTEE AND AFTER-SALE SERVICE

2.6.1 Chainway represents and guarantees to the Partner that the Products comply with the provisions in force, which apply to them, as well as with their own statements regarding them. Chainway will comply with her security and conformity obligations regarding the Products.

2.6.2 Guarantee: Chainway warrants that each of the Products will be free from any defects.

2.6.3 Technical Support: Chainway provides the Partner a more detailed and accurate technical material in electronic edition in English.

2.6.4 Technical training and services support: Chainway has the obligation to train the Partner’s after-sale technical personnel and repairing engineers online. 

2.6.5 Chainway offers free spare parts as a 1-year warranty compensation based on the order pricing category:

(a) Standard Pricing Orders: Chainway offers 1% of the total order value in free spare parts. For example, when the Partner places a standard order of 10,000 USD, Chainway will offer 100 USD equal value of free spare parts, taking it as the fulfillment of the warranty duty.

(b) Special Pricing Orders: For orders with special discounts or promotional prices, Chainway offers 0.5% of the total order value in free spare parts.

2.7 – SAMPLE TESTING AND PROMOTING

For promoting the brand and showing samples to the clients, the Partner must be willing to purchase Chainway products no less than 30 pcs, which could be divided into 6-10 different series/models within the first 1 week from the date this agreement is signed. Chainway shall wait for Partner’s confirmation about the shipment arrangement.

2.8 – SALE TARGET

The sales target for the first year is set at 1,000 units (Accumulated volume).

The Parties agree that this Agreement serves as a preliminary authorization. Upon successfully achieving the sales target of 1,000 units within the validity period, the Parties shall proceed to sign a formal Distributor Agreement for the subsequent term, which may include further favorable terms or authorized territory protections.

3 – TRADEMARK LICENCE

3.1 – REPRESENTATIONS AND GUARANTEES BY Chainway

Chainway represents and guarantees to the Partner that it holds all the intellectual property rights related to the Products and to the Trademark, that it does not harm the rights of third parties in this respect, and that it may validly grant a license to the Partner allowing the Partner to exploit these rights, in order to distribute the Products.

3.2 – COUNTERFEITING, PARASITISM AND UNFAIR COMPETITION

The Partner shall inform Chainway without delay of any counterfeit of the Products or of the Trademark, any attempt to benefit unlawfully from them, any unfair trading or any other unlawful practice likely to attempt to the Products or the Trademark, in the Territory, of which he would become aware, and shall provide his help to Chainway, if this latter so requests, in any action Chainway may undertake to stop such attempts.

4 – FINANCIAL TERMS

Chainway will sell to the Partner the Products and the related accessories(together the « Goods ») as defined below.

4.1 – SELLING PRICE

A – PRICE OF THE PRODUCTS

Chainway shall sell the Products to the Partner at a price expressed in US Dollar taxes excluded, determined as SCHEDULE A. The Partner shall determine freely his resale prices.

B – PRICE OF THE SPARE PARTS

Chainway shall sell spare parts to the Partner the at the pricing agreed upon and accepted by both parties.

C – PRICE OF PROJECT

The Partner has the right to apply for special price for extremely important cases when it is necessary.

4.2 – PAYMENT TERMS

The invoices for Chainway shall be payable in US Dollars.

Method: 30% advance payment, and production will be carried out after receiving the advance payment. Partner is required to complete the remaining 70% of the final payment prior to shipment.

5 – ORDER ADN LEADING TIME

5.1 – LEADING TIME

Chainway undertakes to supply the Partner according to his Orders, within 7–35 working days from each Order, provided that the Partner has duly fulfilled all his contractual and notably payment obligations under this Agreement. Chainway may refuse to deliver Orders in case of any contractual breach by the Partner.

5.2 – CONFIRMATION OF ORDERS

The quantities, prices and shipment of the commodities stated in this Agreement shall be confirmed for each transaction, the particulars of which are to be specified in the Sales Confirmation signed by the two parties hereto.

5.3 – INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

After receiving the Goods, The Partner shall make without delay any observations and reservations that would be appropriate towards the carrier and quality of products and inform Chainway in writing. In the absence of written notification sent to Chainway at least seven[7] calendar days after the receipt of the Goods by the Partner, it shall be presumed that the Goods were provided to the Partner in perfect condition.

Within fifteen [15] days after the receipt of such notice, Chainway will investigate the claim of shortages, or defects, inform Partner of its findings, and deliver to Partner Products to replace any which Chainway determines at its own cost, in its sole discretion, were in short supply, defective at the time of delivery. 

6 – SPECIFIC UNDERTAKINGS

6.1 – REGULATIONS

The Partner hereby commits to run his business in full compliance with any rules, laws, and regulations in force in the Territory, to respect any rights of third parties, and to obtain and maintain any licenses, authorizations, or registrations, which may be needed in the Territory, and to proceed with any declaration that may be required.

The Partner shall inform and advise Chainway as to any rules applicable in the Territory, notably as regards the importing and marketing of the Goods, the legal and contractual guarantees, and the after-sales service.

The Partner holds Chainway harmless against all the consequences of any violations of rules of all kinds whatsoever applying in the Territory.

6.2 – CONFIDENTIALITY

The Partner shall, during the enforcement of the Agreement and after it’s expiry, keep all the terms of this agreement confidential, and shall not disclose to any third party any information contained in the Agreement or any information concerning the business of Chainway, notably the Trademark, the Products, the Goods and Chainway’s distribution network, except where required by law and where needed to implement the Agreement.

The Partner undertakes to attend to the observation of this obligation by his employees, agents, service providers or partners whatsoever, and shall guarantee Chainway in this respect.

7 – DURATION OF THE AGREEMENT

7.1 The Agreement shall enter into force as at the date when all the Parties sign it.

7.2 This agreement when duly signed by the parties concerned shall remain in force for one year to be effective from 21th Jan, 2026 to 20th Jan, 2027.

7.3 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either Party provides written notice of its intention not to renew at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. The sales targets for each renewal term shall be mutually agreed upon in writing by both Parties as an amendment to this Agreement prior to the commencement of such renewal term.

8 – RULES GOVERNING THE AGREEMENT

8.1 – LAW AND JURISDICTION

All disputes arising from the performance of this agreement shall be settled through friendly negotiation. Should no settlement be reached through negotiation, the case shall then be submitted for arbitration to the China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission (Beijing) and the rules of this Commission shall be applied. The award of the arbitration shall be final and binding upon both parties.　

8.2 – NATURE OF THE CONTRACTUAL RELATIONSHIP

This Agreement shall not in any way constitute a work agreement, since the Parties expressly refuse any employment relationship as an essential condition without which they would not have entered into this Agreement.

The Parties expressly state that it is not in their common intent that the responsibilities of a Party, including the employment contracts in force with its employees, may be transferred anyhow from a Party to another Party, in any circumstances whatsoever. 

9 – OTHER TERMS & CONDITIONS

9.1 Force Majeure           

Either party shall not be held responsible for failure or delay to perform all or any part of this agreement due to flood, fire, earthquake, draught, war or any other events which could not be predicted, controlled, avoided or overcome by the relative party.  However, the party affected by the event of Force Majeure shall inform the other party of its occurrence in writing as soon as possible and thereafter send a certificate of the event issued by the relevant authorities to the other party within 15 days after its occurrence.

9.2 Claims           

9.2.1 Chainway does not accept returns, except for product quality issues. In case of detecting the contradiction to the confirmed goods, the quantity, and the quality based on the present Contract during accepting the Goods, the Partner shall have a right to demand to count the cost of the Goods not corresponding to the listed conditions towards the next order or shipment. Together with the claim, the Partner is obliged to offer a survey report of the International Industry and Trade Bureau. 

9.2.2 If the part of non-conformity is not negotiable, the Partner has a right of refusal based on the deficiency in the quality and quantity and can ask the Chainway to compensate 1 by 1 component for replacement.

9.3 – SEVERABILITY, ADAPTATION AND AMENDMENT

In case any provision whatsoever of the Agreement is deemed void, this provision shall not apply but the other provisions of the Agreement shall remain into force.

The Agreement may not be amended except by express and written mutual agreement of the Parties, in which case any amendment or waiver of any provision hereof shall be attached to the Agreement and shall incorporate to it.

9.4 – OFFICIAL DOMICILE

For the purpose of this Agreement, the Parties take up residence in their respective domicile or registered office as indicated in the headings hereto. Any modification shall be notified to the other Party by letter with acknowledgment of receipt or otherwise in writing provided that the other Party duly and expressly acknowledges receipt, in order to be valid.
