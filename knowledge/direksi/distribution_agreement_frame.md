Chainway hereby appoints the Distributor as its non-exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territories, and grants Distributor the right to market, offer to sell, sell and distribute the Products in the Territories based on the conditions of the Agreement.

2.1 – MODE OF DISTRIBUTION

The Distributor will purchase firmly the Products directly from Chainway and resale them retail including but not limited to software houses, system integrators, retailers, or final clients. The Distributor will therefore invoice directly to customers in his own name.

Distributor从Chainway直接购买产品并转售，包括但不限于软件公司、系统集成商、零售商或最终客户。 Distributor将以自己的名义直接向客户开具发票。

2.2 – TERRITORY

Chainway authorizes the Distributor to distribute the Products in the Territory, during the duration of the Agreement.

Chainway授权Distributor在协议期限内在分销区域内分销产品。

2.3 – MARKETING

2.3.1 Chainway's Responsibilities

Chainway shall invest not less than 1 million USD into digital marketing & international exhibitions, including labor, design, websites, promotional materials, videos, B2B, SEO, exhibitions, on-site support which shall be considered in Chainway’s Global markets.

Chainway将持续进行全球市场产品推广，包括网络营销、人工成本、设计费用、网站建设维护、宣传材料、视频、B2B、搜索引擎、展会、现场支持。

Chainway will provide the Distributor for free with the contents, images, and texts relating to the Trademark and the Products in her possession.

Chainway 将免费向Distributor提供与其拥有的商标和产品相关的内容、图像和文本。

2.3.2 Distributor’s Responsibilities

The Distributor shall bear all expenses for advertising and publicity within the aforementioned territory in the duration of this Agreement and submit to Chainway all patterns and/or drawings and descriptions for prior approval.

在本协议有效期内，Distributor在上述经销地区内所作广告宣传的一切费用，由Distributor自理。Distributor须事先向Chainway提供宣传广告的图案及文字说明，由Chainway审阅同意。 

The Distributor acknowledges that any action, promotion, canvassing or communication relating to Chainway or to the Products shall respect their image and their positioning and shall comply with Chainway’s quality standards as well as with its marketing policy. The Distributor will comply with all the obligations applying to his activities and will in particular ensure that the distribution of the Products and his promotion actions are always perfectly lawful and appropriate considering the aim pursued, in order to preserve the reputation and the image of Chainway and of the Products.

Distributor承认，与Chainway或产品相关的任何行动、促销、游说或交流都应尊重其形象和定位，并应符合Chainway的质量标准及其营销政策。 Distributor在产品的分销及其促销活动过程中，需合法合规，以维护品牌的声誉和形象和产品。

2.4 – INTERNET

The Distributor must have its official commercial website where Chainway products can be displayed, the Distributor shall prepare fully localized promotional materials including printing brochures, catalogues, posters, and electronic datasheets.

The Distributor必须有能展示Chainway产品的官方网站，能制作印刷本地化宣传材料（包括彩页、目录及海报）、能制作本地化语言电子文档。

The Distributor shall obtain the authorization of Chainway as to the plan and the content of the Pages, prior to their initial publishing, their modification, or update.

Distributor应在页面的计划和内容首次发布、修改或更新之前获得 Chainway 的授权。

The Distributor shall bear all the costs related to these Pages, notably development, hosting, referencing, search engine optimization, and exploitation.

Distributor应承担与这些页面相关的所有费用，特别是开发、托管、参考、搜索引擎优化和利用。

2.5 – SALES REPORTS

In order to keep Chainway well informed of the prevailing market conditions, the Distributor should undertake to supply Chainway, at least once a quarter or at any time when necessary, with market reports concerning changes of the local regulations in connection with the import and sales of the commodity covered by this agreement, local market tendency and the Distributor's comments on quality, packing, price, etc. of the goods supplied by Chainway under this agreement. 

为使Chaiwnay充分了解现行市场情况，the Distributor承担至少每季度一次或在必要时随时向Chainway提供市场报告， 内容包括与本协议代理商品的进口与销售有关的地方规章的变动、当地市场发展趋势以及the Distributor对Chainway按协议供应的货物的品质、包装、价格等方面的意见。

2.6 – SECURITY, CONFORMITY, GUARANTEE AND AFTER-SALE SERVICE

2.6.1 Chainway represents and guarantees to the Distributor that the Products comply with the provisions in force, which apply to them, as well as with their own statements regarding them. Chainway will comply with her security and conformity obligations regarding the Products.

Chainway 向Distributor声明并保证产品及其声明符合现行行业规定。 Chainway 将遵守其关于产品的安全性和合规性义务。

2.6.2 Guarantee: Chainway warrants that each of the Products will be free from any defects.

保证：Chainway保证每件产品均无任何缺陷。

2.6.3 Technical Support: Chainway provides the Distributor a more detailed and accurate technical material in electronic edition in English.

技术资料支持：Chainway应向Distributor提供较为详细和准确的技术资料英语版本电子版.

2.6.4 Technical training and services support: Chainway has the obligation to train the Distributor’s after-sale technical personnel and repairing engineers online. 

技术培训和服务支持：Chainway有义务对Distributor的售后技术人员及维修工程师进行线上培训。

2.6.5 Chainway offers a 1% free spare part as a 1-year warranty compensation. For example, when the Distributor places an order of 10000 USD, Chainway will offer 100 USD equal value of free spare parts, taking it as the fulfillment of the warranty duty. 

Chainway 提供 1% 的免费备件作为 1 年保修补偿。 例如，当经销商订购10000美元时，Chainway将提供等值100美元的免费备件，视为履行了保修义务。

The distributor is held responsible for all warranty work on behalf of Chainway.

Distributor代表Chainway完成所有保修工作。

For the reason of saving time and money for shipping and manpower, the free spare parts will ship with normal orders.

出于节省运输和人力的时间和金钱的原因，免费备件将随正常订单一起发货。

For promoting the brand and showing samples to the clients, the distributor must be willing to purchase Chainway products no less than 50 pcs, which could be divided into 6-10 different series/models within the first 1 week from the date this agreement is signed. Chainway shall wait for Distributor’s confirmation about the shipment arrangement.

为了向客户宣传品牌和展示样品，Distributor必须愿意在本协议签订之日起1周内购买成为成威产品不少于50件，可分为6-10个不同的系列/型号 。 成威将等待Distributor确认发货安排。

2.9.1 The sales target(expectation) / commission of the Distributor will be negotiated from the second year as an amendment to the Agreement

双方都理解这不是强制性义务，而是需要双方配合竭力完成的目标。

订单的确认: 关于协议所规定的上述商品的每笔交易，其数量、价格及装运条件等须经双方确认，并签定销售确认书，对交易做具体规定。

在收到此类通知后的十五天内，Chainway 将调查短缺或缺陷的索赔，将其调查结果通知Distributor，并自费向Distributor更换短缺或缺陷的产品。
