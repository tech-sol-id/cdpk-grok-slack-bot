3	APPOINTMENT OF DISTRIBUTOR

3.1	The Manufacturer appoints the Distributor as its distributor to import, market and distribute the Goods in the Territories during the Contract Period.

3.2	The Contract Period shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall continue for such period as stated in Clause 1 (or such other period as agreed in writing between the Distributor and the Manufacturer) unless terminated earlier:

(a)	as provided by the terms of this Agreement; or

(b)	by either Party giving to the other prior written notice of not less than the period as stated in Clause 1.

3.3	The Distributor will not:

(a)	; or

(b)	sell, distribute or otherwise make available the Goods to distributors, resellers or end-users outside the Territories.

3.4	The Distributor will not:

(a)	represent itself as an agent of the Manufacturer for any purpose;

(b)	give any warranty or make any representation or any commitment on behalf of the Manufacturer; or

(c)	incur any liability for or on behalf of the Manufacturer.

3.5	Notwithstanding Clause 3.1, the Manufacturer reserves the right, based on business requirements, to designate authorized dealers to enter into commercial contracts with end-users in the Territories. Such authorized dealers shall procure all Goods through the Distributor. The Distributor shall facilitate the supply chain for all such transactions and shall not unreasonably withhold or delay the supply of Goods to Manufacturer-designated authorized dealers.

3.6	The Manufacturer may, at its discretion and based on project circumstances, provide product price discounts and support for specific projects or end-users. Such discounts and support shall be channeled through the Distributor to the designated dealers or end-users. Upon receiving discount support from the Manufacturer, the Distributor shall pass through the full discount to the market (dealers or end-users) without retention. Where the discount applies to Goods already purchased and held in inventory by the Distributor, the corresponding discount amount shall be credited against the Distributor’s next purchase order from the Manufacturer. The Distributor and the Manufacturer shall, when necessary, jointly provide the required support to ensure project success.

3.7	The Parties acknowledge that they have separately entered into a Memorandum of Understanding dated [date] (the “MOU”) which sets forth the strategic cooperation framework, market development principles, roles and responsibilities, and business conduct guidelines for the Indonesian market. The market cooperation principles, roles and responsibilities, and strategic objectives set forth in the MOU shall be read in conjunction with this Agreement. In the event of any inconsistency between this Agreement and the MOU on matters relating to product supply, pricing, delivery, warranties, and other commercial execution terms, this Agreement shall prevail. In the event of any inconsistency on matters relating to market cooperation principles, roles and responsibilities, business conduct, and strategic objectives, the MOU shall prevail.

4	SUPPLY OF GOODS

4.1	The Distributor will purchase the Goods for its own account for resale.

4.4	Each Order is a separate offer by the Distributor to purchase Goods on and subject to the terms of this Agreement. The Manufacturer may accept or decline Orders at its discretion provided that the Manufacturer must use all reasonable endeavors to meet Orders to the extent of the forecasted amount for the relevant period.

6	DELIVERY

6.1	The Goods will be delivered to the Delivery Location according to such delivery dates or schedule as agreed by the Distributor. Delivery dates are estimation only and the time of delivery is not of the essence. Upon giving reasonable notice to the Distributor, the Manufacturer may deliver the Goods prior to the Delivery Date.

6.2	Delivery is completed on the completion of unloading of the Goods at the Delivery Location.

6.3	The Manufacturer will not be liable for any delay or failure in delivery of the Goods if the delay or failure is caused by the Distributor’s failure to provide accurate delivery instructions.

7	DISTRIBUTOR’S UNDERTAKING

The Distributor undertakes that at all times during the Contract Period it will:

(a)	use its best endeavors to promote the distribution and sale of the Goods in the Territories;

(b)	regularly report to the Manufacturer on the marketing and sales of the Goods;

(c)	keep proper books of account and records of all transactions and proceedings relating to the Goods with sufficient clarity, and allow the Manufacturer access to such accounts and records upon giving reasonable notice;

(d)	provide after-sale repair and maintenance services in respect of the Goods;

(e)	keep stocks of the Goods in appropriate conditions and adopt reasonable security measures in respect thereof; and

(f)	allow representatives or agents of the Manufacturer access to the premises of the Distributor for inspection upon giving reasonable notice and at reasonable hours.

(g)	assign one (1) dedicated sales person from the Distributor’s team to support the Manufacturer’s local Sales Manager in jointly achieving business objectives, in accordance with the roles and responsibilities defined in the MOU. The assigned sales person shall focus on mid-to-late stage commercial liaison, customer relationship building, and post-sales engagement, and shall be directly responsible for converting the Distributor’s existing customers into new business opportunities for the Goods.

8	MANUFACTURER’S UNDERTAKING

The Manufacturer undertakes that at all times during the Contract Period it will:

(a)	use all reasonable endeavors to supply Goods to the Distributor for resale in the Territories to the extent of the Distributor’s forecasted amounts;

(b)	supply any spare parts for the Goods requested by the Distributor, subject to availability;

(c)	provide reasonable assistance in the promotion of the Goods in the Territories upon request of the Distributor; and

(d)	provide sufficient training for the representatives of the Distributor in relation to the installation, use, sale, maintenance and repair (where applicable) of the Goods.

(e)	establish and communicate product pricing frameworks to the Distributor, and coordinate with the Distributor on project planning, delivery schedules, and customer engagement strategies in accordance with the MOU.

The Distributor will be given a reasonable opportunity of comparing the bulk with the sample and the Manufacturer warrants that the Goods will be free from any defect which would not be apparent on reasonable examination of the sample.

10	TITLE AND RISK

10.1	Risk in the Goods will pass to the Distributor upon unloading the Goods at the Delivery Location.

10.2	Title to the Goods will not pass to the Distributor until the earlier of:

(a)	full settlement of the Purchase Price; or

(b)	resale of the Goods in which case Clause 10.3 will apply.

10.3	Subject to Clause 10.4, the Distributor may resell or use (in its ordinary course of business) the Goods before the Purchase Price has been settled in full, in which case:

(a)	the Distributor resells the Goods as principal and not as an agent of the Manufacturer; and

(b)	title to the Goods will pass from the Manufacturer to the Distributor immediately before the time at which the resale occurs.

10.4	Where any of the events under Clause 16.1(d) to 16.1(h) occurs to the Distributor:

(a)	the Distributor’s right to resell or use the Goods under Clause 10.3 will cease immediately; and

(b)	the Manufacturer may at any time require the Distributor to return all Goods in its possession that have not been resold, or where the Distributor fails to do so promptly, enter into the Distributor’s premises or of any third party to retrieve the Goods to be returned.

10.5	Until title to the Goods passes to the Distributor, the Distributor undertakes:

(a)	to store the Goods separately from the Distributor’s other goods such that the Goods remain readily identifiable as the Manufacturer’s property;

(b)	not to remove, deface or obscure any identification mark or package of the Goods; and

(c)	to maintain the Goods in satisfactory condition.

10.6	The Distributor licenses and permits the Manufacturer and any of its officers, employees or agents to enter any premises of the Distributor to recover any Goods the title of which has not passed to the Distributor.

11	PURCHASE PRICE

11.1	The Purchase Price shall be determined based on the Manufacturer’s recommended market price list (as set out in the Price Schedule attached as an appendix to this Agreement), subject to a distribution discount of thirty-five percent (35%) off the listed market price (i.e., the Distributor’s purchase price shall be sixty-five percent (65%) of the listed market price). The Purchase Price:

excludes goods and services tax, value added tax or any other applicable taxes, which (if any) shall be invoiced to or paid for directly by the Distributor at the prevailing rate.

Shipping Terms: CFR (Cost and Freight) Jakarta, Indonesia. The Purchase Price includes the cost of sea freight from Singapore to Jakarta port. The Distributor shall be responsible for all import clearance procedures, customs duties, import taxes, port handling charges, inland transportation, and any other costs incurred from the point of arrival at Jakarta port to the final delivery destination.

11.2	The Manufacturer will charge the Distributor for the Goods ordered according to the pricing framework described in Clause 11.1, as notified to the Distributor from time to time.

11.4	Distributor Annual Performance Rebate: The Manufacturer shall provide an annual performance rebate to the Distributor based on the Distributor’s cumulative purchase payments during each calendar year, subject to the following conditions: 

If the Distributor’s total qualifying purchase payments reach USD 500,000, the Manufacturer shall grant a rebate of two percent (2%) of the qualifying purchase payment amount. 

If the Distributor’s total qualifying purchase payments reach USD 1,000,000, the Manufacturer shall grant a rebate of three percent (3%) of the qualifying purchase payment amount. 

The rebate shall be calculated based on actual payment amounts received by the Manufacturer from the Distributor during the calendar year. 

Products sold at special discounted prices (i.e., prices below the standard distribution price set forth in Clause 11.1) shall be excluded from the rebate calculation. However, the payment amounts for such special discounted products shall be counted towards the qualifying threshold amounts of USD 500,000 or USD 1,000,000 for the purpose of determining rebate eligibility. 

The rebate shall be settled in the form of Goods (product credit), not cash. The Manufacturer shall deliver the rebate Goods to the Distributor during the first quarter (Q1) of the following calendar year. The specific products and quantities shall be agreed between the Parties based on the Distributor’s requirements and the Manufacturer’s product availability.

11.5	Dealer Performance Rebate: In addition to the Distributor’s rebate under Clause 11.4, the Manufacturer may establish separate annual performance rebate programs for authorized dealers (“Dealers”) who procure Goods through the Distributor. Such rebate programs shall be subject to the following terms: 

Rebate thresholds, rates, and qualifying conditions for Dealers shall be determined by the Manufacturer and communicated to the Distributor and the relevant Dealers in writing. The Dealer rebate is separate from and in addition to the Distributor’s rebate under Clause 11.4. 

The Dealer rebate shall be calculated based on actual payment amounts attributable to each Dealer’s purchases through the Distributor during the calendar year. 

Products sold at special discounted prices shall be excluded from the Dealer rebate calculation, but the payment amounts for such products shall be counted towards the qualifying threshold for determining Dealer rebate eligibility. 

The Dealer rebate shall be settled in the form of Goods (product credit), not cash. The Manufacturer shall deliver the Dealer rebate Goods to the Distributor during the first quarter (Q1) of the following calendar year, at no cost to the Distributor. 

The Distributor shall transfer the Dealer rebate Goods to the corresponding Dealer in full within thirty (30) days of receipt from the Manufacturer. The Distributor shall not retain, substitute, delay, or otherwise withhold any portion of the Dealer rebate Goods. 

The Distributor’s own rebate under Clause 11.4 and the Dealer rebate under this Clause 11.5 are calculated independently and do not affect each other.

16	TERMINATION

16.1	Without prejudice to any accrued rights or remedies available to it, either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by giving written notice to the other Party if at any time a Party:

commits any serious or repeated breach of any of the provisions of this Agreement;

(in the case of the Distributor) fails to pay the Purchase Price when they fall due for more than one (1) month(s);

suspends or ceases to carry on all or a substantial part of its business;

suspends or is unable to pay its debts when they fall due;

is (where the Party is an individual) declared bankrupt or makes any arrangement with or for the benefit of his creditors;

is (where the Party is an individual) incapable of performing his obligations under this Agreement (by reason of illness or incapacity or otherwise) for an extended period; or

is (where the Party is an entity) a petition is filed, a notice is given, a resolution is passed, or an order is made, for or in connection with the winding up of the Party (other than for the sole purpose of a scheme for a solvent amalgamation or solvent restructuring).

16.2	Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the Parties that have accrued up to the date of termination or expiry, including the right to claim damages in respect of any breach of the Agreement which existed at or before the date of termination or expiry.

16.3	On termination the Manufacturer will have the option to buy back all or part of the stocks of the Goods at the same price at which the Distributor purchased them. Such option must be exercised within one (undefined) month(s) from the effective date of termination by giving notice in writing to the Distributor, specifying the quantity of Goods the Manufacturer intends to buy back. Within two (2) week(s) from such notice, the Distributor must deliver the relevant quantity of Goods to the Manufacturer.

16.4	Where the Manufacturer chooses not to exercise its option to buy back all or any of the Goods or fails to exercise its right under Clause 16.3 within the specified period, the Distributor shall be free to sell or distribute any remaining stocks of the Goods under its control, or dispose of the remaining stocks as the Manufacturer directs.

17	FORCE MAJEURE

17.1	Neither Party shall be in breach of this Agreement or be liable for delay in performing, or failure to perform, any of its obligations under this Agreement if such delay or failure result from events, circumstances or causes beyond its reasonable control. In such circumstances, the time for performance shall be extended by a period equivalent to the period during which performance of the obligation has been delayed or failed to be performed.

17.2	A force majeure event includes: an act of God, fire, flood, typhoon, storm, war, riot, civil unrest, act of terrorism, strike, industrial dispute, major economic recession, outbreak of epidemic or pandemic illness, or failure of utility service or transportation.

17.3	If the period of delay or non-performance continues for twelve (12) month(s), the Party not affected may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice of one (1) month(s) to the affected Party (or whether both Parties are affected by the force majeure event, either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice of one (1) month(s) to the other Party).
