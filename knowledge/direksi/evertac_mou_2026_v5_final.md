MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

2026 Strategic Cooperation Framework

Between Evertac Solutions Singapore Pte Ltd and the Designated Local Distributor

Preamble

This Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”) is entered into by and between Evertac Solutions Singapore Pte Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of the Republic of Singapore (hereinafter referred to as “Evertac”) and [Company Name], a company incorporated under the laws of the Republic of Indonesia, mutually designated by both Evertac and [Distributor Company Name] as the local distribution entity for Evertac products in Indonesia (hereinafter referred to as the “Distributor”), collectively referred to as the “Parties.”

WHEREAS, Evertac and [Distributor Company Name] signed a Strategic Cooperation Agreement in 2025 to jointly develop the Indonesian market for two-way radio and communication solutions;

WHEREAS, following consultations between the Parties, both sides have clarified the issues arising from the 2025 cooperation and have agreed to address sensitive matters of principle in this new agreement framework;

WHEREAS, the Parties have mutually agreed that the signing counterpart for this MOU shall no longer be [Distributor Company Name] directly, but rather a new company mutually designated by both sides to serve as the local distribution entity;

WHEREAS, authorized product pricing shall be directly executed between Evertac Solutions Singapore Pte Ltd and the Distributor;

WHEREAS, Evertac is incorporated and domiciled in Singapore and does not maintain a registered business entity or operational presence in the Republic of Indonesia, and accordingly requires a local partner to execute commercial transactions within Indonesia in compliance with Indonesian law;

WHEREAS, both Parties wish to formalize a clear and transparent cooperation framework for 2026, defining the responsibilities and division of work between them;

WHEREAS, the Parties have separately entered into a Distribution Agreement dated [DA Date] (the “Distribution Agreement”) governing the commercial terms of product supply, pricing, delivery, warranties, and related matters;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree to the following terms and principles:

Article 1 — Cooperation Framework and Principles

The following principles shall form the foundation of all business activities conducted jointly or individually by the Parties in the Indonesian market:

1.1 Market Access Principle — Evertac shall not directly enter into binding commercial contracts with end-users in Indonesia. All commercial contracts shall be executed through the Distributor or through locally authorized dealers designated by Evertac based on business needs. Evertac retains the right to provide suggested pricing (Manufacturer’s Reference Quotation or Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price) to end-users, but shall inform end-users that all commercial execution will be carried out by the local authorized distributor or designated dealer.

1.2 Pricing Authority — Authorized distribution pricing for Evertac products shall be directly agreed and signed between Evertac Solutions Singapore Pte Ltd and the Distributor. The Distributor shall adhere to the pricing framework established by Evertac. The specific purchase price terms and conditions shall be governed by the Distribution Agreement.

1.3 Non-Distributor Customer Channels — For customers that are not sourced by the Distributor, Evertac may, based on business requirements, designate authorized dealers to sign contracts with end-users. Such dealers shall procure products through the Distributor. The Distributor shall facilitate the supply chain for all such transactions.

1.4 Pricing Discounts and Market Support — Evertac may, at its discretion and based on project circumstances, provide product price discounts and support to end-users for Evertac products. Such discounts and support shall be channeled through the Distributor to the designated dealers or end-users. Upon receiving discount support from Evertac, the Distributor is required to pass through the full discount to the market (dealers or end-users) without retention. When necessary, the Distributor and Evertac shall jointly provide the required support to ensure project success.

1.5 Transparency and Communication — Both Parties shall establish a transparent and timely information-sharing mechanism on all projects. Project success shall be the primary objective guiding all decisions and actions by both Parties.

Article 2 — Roles and Responsibilities

The division of work between the Parties for 2026 shall be as follows:

2.1 Distributor’s Responsibilities:

(a) Identify and provide potential customers and business opportunities in the Indonesian market to Evertac.

(b) Assign one (1) dedicated sales person from the Distributor’s team to support Evertac’s local Sales Manager in jointly achieving business objectives.

(c) The Distributor’s assigned sales person shall focus on mid-to-late stage commercial liaison, customer relationship building, and post-sales engagement with clients.

(d) The Distributor’s sales person shall be directly responsible for converting the Distributor’s existing customers into new business opportunities for Evertac products.

(e) Execute commercial contracts with end-users or facilitate supply chain transactions as required.

(f) Ensure compliance with all Indonesian regulatory requirements, including TA (Type Approval) certification for Evertac products.

(g) Complete necessary import and registration procedures in a timely manner to avoid delays in business delivery.

2.2 Evertac’s Responsibilities:

(a) Evertac’s Sales Manager in Indonesia shall focus on market development, brand building, and project strategy for Evertac products.

(b) Provide technical support, solution design, and pre-sales consultation to end-users and project stakeholders.

(c) Establish and communicate product pricing frameworks to the Distributor.

(d) Provide product training, technical documentation, and marketing materials to support the Distributor’s sales efforts.

(e) Coordinate with the Distributor on project planning, delivery schedules, and customer engagement strategies.

Article 3 — Product Scope

The product scope covered under this MOU includes the following Evertac products and solutions:

3.1 Commercial Channel Repeaters (Trunking Repeaters and Conventional Repeaters)

3.2 Combiner Platforms (Multi-Coupler Systems)

3.3 OMU (Optical Master Unit) and OUR (Optical Remote Unit) products

3.4 DAS (Distributed Antenna System) products

3.5 Portable and Mobile Two-Way Radios

3.6 Software Products (including dispatch, management, and application software)

The Distributor shall be authorized to market, sell, and distribute the above product categories in Indonesia in accordance with the terms of this MOU and the Distribution Agreement.

Article 4 — 2026 Business Objectives

4.1 Evertac plans to develop and deliver USD 500,000 to USD 1,000,000 in business value in the Indonesian market during 2026.

4.2 Evertac intends for these business deals to be supplied and/or executed through the Distributor, enabling the Distributor to directly benefit from market growth.

4.3 Both Parties acknowledge that this business target represents a long-term anchor point for market share development, rather than short-term profit maximization.

Article 5 — Market Focus Areas

Evertac shall increase its market investment in Indonesia in 2026, focusing on the following key markets:

5.1 Data Centers — Communication solutions designed for data center environments.

5.2 Commercial Buildings — Two-way radio and communication solutions for commercial building projects.

5.3 Consultants and End Users — Deepening partnerships with engineering consultant firms and direct engagement with end-user clients.

The Distributor shall cooperate with Evertac in the above market development efforts.

Article 6 — Execution Standards

For all business transferred by Evertac to the Distributor for execution or supply, the following standards shall apply:

6.2 Execution Quality — All business execution transferred from Evertac must meet Evertac’s operational standards. The service strategy defined by Evertac shall take precedence in all client-facing activities.

6.3 Client Satisfaction — Both Parties agree that client delivery satisfaction is the highest priority. Failure to meet delivery timelines or quality standards may result in review and adjustment of the cooperation terms.

Article 7 — Principal’s Reserved Rights

As the product principal, technology developer, and brand owner, Evertac retains the following rights:

7.1 Brand and Intellectual Property Ownership — Evertac retains full ownership and control over the Evertac brand, trademarks, product names, technical documentation, and all associated intellectual property. The terms of trademark usage shall be as set forth in the Distribution Agreement.

7.2 Product and Technology Authority — Evertac holds sole authority over product definition, technical specifications, solution architecture, feature roadmaps, and quality standards.

7.3 Direct Technical Engagement — Evertac reserves the right to engage directly with end-users and project stakeholders for technical consultation, solution design, product demonstration, and pre-sales technical support, in coordination with the Distributor.

7.4 Market Presence and Industry Participation — Evertac reserves the right to participate in industry exhibitions, trade shows, technical seminars, and market development activities in Indonesia.

7.5 Partnership Governance — Evertac reserves the right to conduct periodic reviews of market performance, customer satisfaction, and partnership alignment.

Article 8 — Business Conduct Principles

8.1 Evertac shall not independently enter into any binding commercial contract directly with any end-user in Indonesia. All commercial execution shall be channeled through the Distributor or through Evertac-designated authorized dealers who shall procure through the Distributor. 

8.3 The obligations under this Article shall remain in effect for the duration of this MOU and for a period of twelve (12) months following its termination or expiration with respect to any business opportunities initiated during the term of this MOU.

Article 9 — Confidentiality

Both Parties shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all business information, pricing strategies, client details, and internal communications shared during the course of this cooperation. The detailed confidentiality obligations as set forth in Clause 15 of the Distribution Agreement shall apply. This obligation shall survive the termination or expiration of this MOU.

Article 10 — Term and Review

10.1 This MOU shall be effective from the date of execution by both Parties and shall remain in effect for the calendar year 2026, unless extended or terminated by mutual written agreement.

10.2 The Parties shall conduct a mid-year review meeting to assess progress against the objectives outlined herein and to make any necessary adjustments to the cooperation framework.

10.3 Either Party may propose modifications to this MOU by providing written notice, subject to mutual agreement.

Article 11 — Relationship with Distribution Agreement

11.1 This MOU sets forth the strategic cooperation framework, market development principles, roles and responsibilities, and business conduct guidelines between the Parties for the Indonesian market. The Distribution Agreement governs the commercial terms of product supply, including but not limited to purchase pricing, minimum purchase obligations, delivery, warranties, title and risk, and termination.

11.2 In the event of any inconsistency between this MOU and the Distribution Agreement on matters relating to product supply, pricing, delivery, warranties, and other commercial execution terms, the Distribution Agreement shall prevail.

11.3 In the event of any inconsistency between this MOU and the Distribution Agreement on matters relating to market cooperation principles, roles and responsibilities, business conduct, and strategic objectives, this MOU shall prevail.

11.4 The Parties acknowledge that this MOU and the Distribution Agreement are complementary documents that together form the complete framework for their cooperation in the Indonesian market. Both documents shall be read and interpreted in a manner consistent with each other to the fullest extent possible.

Article 12 — General Provisions

12.1 This MOU represents the mutual intent and understanding of the Parties regarding their strategic cooperation in the Indonesian market for 2026.

12.2 This MOU does not create any legally binding obligations unless and until a formal agreement is executed by both Parties. However, both Parties commit to acting in good faith in accordance with the principles outlined herein.

12.3 Any disputes arising from the interpretation or implementation of this MOU shall be resolved through good-faith negotiation between the Parties. If the Parties are unable to resolve the dispute through negotiation within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in accordance with its prevailing rules. The seat of arbitration shall be Singapore, and the language of arbitration shall be English.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Memorandum of Understanding as of the date set forth below.
