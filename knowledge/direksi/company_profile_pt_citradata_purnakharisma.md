About Us
To Whom It May Concern
It is a pleasure to introduce our company, PT. Citradata Purnakharisma as an exclusive 
distributor for Motorola radio communication equipment. Our continuing growth over the 
last three years has resulted in domination of the Motorola radio communications markets. 
We are well known as Authorized Dealer and Authorized Service Centre, well stocked and 
well equipped, far more superior than our closest competitors. Our company has been 
operating for over 30 years, and we have managed to serve an extensive customer 
throughout Indonesia.
This has been achieved by technical superiority, competitive pricing, great customer 
service, and reliable products.
We are supported by a major communications manufacturing company, Motorola Inc., and 
have access to all of the company’s radio products worldwide, as well as their supporting 
equipment and wide range of accessories.
