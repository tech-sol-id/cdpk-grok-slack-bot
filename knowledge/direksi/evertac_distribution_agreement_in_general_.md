DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT 
THIS AGREEMENT is dated 2nd December 2025 (the “Agreement”).
Parties
1. Evertac Solutions Singapore Pte. Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of Singapore, with its registered office at 18 Boon Lay #03-108 Tradehub 21, Singapore (609966), and UEN 202230146C (the “Supplier”).
2. PT Citradata Purnakharisma, a company incorporated under the laws of Indonesia, with its registered office at Jl. Pinangsia Raya, Komplek Glodok Plaza H-28, Jakarta 11110, Indonesia (the “Distributor”).
Collectively referred to as the "Parties", and individually as a "Party".
Background
The Supplier agrees to appoint the Distributor as its distributor for the promotion and sale of the Goods (as hereinafter defined) in the Territories on the following terms.
Agreed terms
1. APPOINTMENT OF DISTRIBUTOR
1. The Supplier appoints the Distributor as its exclusive distributor to import, market and distribute the Goods in the Territories during the Contract Period.


2. The Distributor will not:


1. sell, distribute or manufacture any goods which compete with the Goods during the Contract Period; or


2. sell, distribute or otherwise make available the Goods to distributors, resellers or end-users outside the Territories.


3. represent itself as an agent of the Supplier for any purpose;


4. give any warranty or make any representation or any commitment on behalf of the Supplier; or


5. incur any liability for or on behalf of the Supplier.
2. DISTRIBUTOR’S UNDERTAKING
1. use its best endeavours to promote the distribution and sale of the Goods in the Territories;


2. regularly report to the Supplier on the marketing and sales of the Goods;


3. keep proper books of account and records of all transactions and proceedings relating to the Goods with sufficient clarity, and allow the Supplier access to such accounts and records upon giving reasonable notice;


4. provide after-sale repair and maintenance services in respect of the Goods;


5. keep stocks of the Goods in appropriate conditions and adopt reasonable security measures in respect thereof; and


6. allow representatives or agents of the Supplier access to the premises of the Distributor for inspection upon giving reasonable notice and at reasonable hours.


3. SUPPLIER’S UNDERSTANDING 
1. use all reasonable endeavours to supply Goods to the Distributor for resale in the Territories to the extent of the Distributor's forecasted amounts;


2. supply any spare parts for the Goods requested by the Distributor, subject to availability;


3. actively support the Distributor’s marketing efforts within the agreed territory; and


4. provide sufficient training for the representatives of the Distributor in relation to the installation, use, sale, maintenance and repair (where applicable) of the Goods.
4. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS AND REGULATIONS
1. Each Party will, at its own cost and expenses, comply with all laws and regulations applicable to the performance of its respective obligations under this Agreement, including any applicable laws, codes or regulations relating to anti-bribery or anti-corruption.


2. The Distributor will obtain all licences or permits and settle any customs duties, clearance charges, taxes, brokers' fees and other amounts payable in connection with the importation and delivery of the Goods.


5. TRADE MARKS
1. The Distributor acknowledges that all rights in the Trade Marks shall remain with the Supplier.


2. The Supplier grants the Distributor, or shall procure the grant to the Distributor, a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use the Trade Marks for the duration of Contract Period to such extent as is necessary for the promotion, advertisement and sale of the Goods. The Distributor shall not sub-license or transfer such right to use the Trade Marks.


3. Each Party will notify the other in writing if it becomes aware of:


      1. any infringement or suspected infringement of the Trade Marks or any other Intellectual Property Rights relating to the Goods; or


      2. any claim that the Goods or the manufacture, sale or use of the Goods within the Territories infringes the rights of any third party.


6. CONFIDENTIALITY
1. Each Party undertakes that it shall not at any time disclose to any person any confidential information (whether or not stated to be confidential or marked as such) concerning the business, affairs, distributors, clients or suppliers of the other Party or of any member of the group of companies to which the other Party belongs, except as permitted by Clause 6.2.


2. Each Party may disclose the other Party's confidential information:


      1. to its employees, officers, representatives or advisers who need to know such information for the purposes of carrying out the Party's obligations under this Agreement. Each Party shall ensure that its employees, officers, representatives or advisers to whom it discloses the other Party's confidential information comply with this clause; and


      2. as may be required by law, a court of competent jurisdiction or any governmental or regulatory authority.


3. No Party shall use any other Party's confidential information for any purpose other than to exercise its rights or perform its obligations under this Agreement.


7. TERM AND TERMINATION
1. This Agreement shall commence on 1st October 2024 and shall remain in effect for a period of 24 (twenty-four) months, unless terminated earlier by either Party with written notice of at least 30 (thirty) days.
2. Upon mutual agreement, this Agreement may be extended for additional terms.
8. FORCE MAJEURE
1. Neither Party shall be considered in breach of this Agreement nor held liable for any delay or failure to perform any of its obligations under this Agreement if such delay or failure is the result of events, circumstances, or causes beyond its reasonable control. In such cases, the timeline for performance shall be extended by a period equal to the duration of the delay or non-performance.


2. A Force Majeure Event includes but is not limited to: acts of God, fire, flood, typhoon, storm, war, riot, civil unrest, act of terrorism, strike, industrial dispute, outbreak of epidemic or pandemic illness, major economic recession, or failure of utility services or transportation systems.


3. If the period of delay or non-performance continues for twelve (12) month(s), the Parties shall engage in good faith renegotiation to determine appropriate adjustments to the Agreement, including but not limited to timelines, responsibilities, or temporary suspension of obligations.


9. VARIATION
No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Parties (or their authorised representatives).


10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
1. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes and extinguishes all previous agreements, promises, assurances, warranties, representations and understandings between them, whether written or oral, relating to its subject matter. 


2. Each Party agrees that it shall have no remedies in respect of any statement, representation, assurance or warranty (whether made innocently or negligently) that is not set out in this Agreement.


11. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Singapore.


12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes, controversies, or differences (the “Dispute”) arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any questions regarding its existence, validity, or termination, shall first be referred to mediation in Singapore, in accordance with the Law Society Mediation Rules for the time being in force. If the Dispute cannot be resolved in mediation, the Parties shall refer the Dispute to arbitration in Singapore in accordance with the Law Society Arbitration Scheme and the rules thereunder for the time being in force.


This Agreement has been entered into on the date stated at the beginning of it.
