A. RETURN MERCHANDISE AUTHORIZATION (RMA) PROCEDURES:  
 
During the warranty period, one (1) year from the date of invoice for theJOYfactory products 
unless otherwise advised by the Master Distributor, the Master Distributor will repair or 
replace at its discretion any defective products. Such procedures (RMA procedures) are 
outlined as follows.  
 
1. RETURNS: RMA number must first be obtained from Master Distributor. To obtain an 
RMA number, Sole Distributor is required to provide the invoice number, shipping date, part 
number, serial number (if available), and the reasons for return. Products returned must be 
received by Master Distributor within fifteen (15) days after issuance of the RMA number. 
For defective products returned, Sole Distributor is required to test and identifies the nature 
of the defect. Should Master Distributor find the products to be non-defective, such products 
will be returned to Sole Distributor freight collect.  
2. SHIPPING: Products should be returned to Master Distributor freight pre-paid in the 
original boxes and packing materials. Returned products must be complete, including all 
manuals, cables, accessories, and etc, otherwise, a charge for the missing items will apply. 
RMA number must be marked clearly outside the shipping carton, and also on the mailing 
labels. Proof of purchase and a note of reasons for return must be enclosed. No freight 
collects or C.O.D. on return RMA shipment will be accepted by Master Distributor. In the 
event that RMA shipment not meeting all the above conditions, Master Distributor reserves 
the right to refuse the shipment and the imposition of handling charges.  
 
3. SERVICES: Master Distributor will return the repaired or replaced products to the 
Sole Distributor at Principle’s cost, provided that the products were originally purchased from 
Master Distributor, and are still covered by the warranty policy, and such products are 
confirmed to be defective.  
 
4. DEFECTIVE ON ARRIVAL (D.O.A.): Products that are reported as D.O.A. by Sole 
Distributor may be returned to Master Distributor for exchange, credit or refund, within fifteen 
(15) days from the invoice day under which the products were shipped.
