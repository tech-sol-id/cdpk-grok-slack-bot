The Joy Factory APAC Appoints TCE as Master Distributor for HazLoc Mobility 
Solutions in Southeast Asia
Advancing Mission-Critical Mobility Solutions for Hazardous Industries
Singapore / Malaysia / Indonesia — [Insert Date]
The Joy Factory APAC (TJF APAC) is pleased to announce the appointment of Technics Communication & 
Electronics (TCE) as the Master Distributor for HazLoc mobility solutions in Southeast Asia, covering 
Singapore, Malaysia, and Indonesia.
To strengthen local market coverage and customer support:
• Triple Access Sdn Bhd is appointed as the sole distributor for Malaysia 
• PT. Citradata Punakharisma is appointed as the sole distributor for Indonesia 
• Together, the partners form a strong regional distribution and support network 
This partnership aims to accelerate the adoption of explosion-proof mobility solutions and connected 
worker technologies across industries such as:
• Oil & Gas 
• Petrochemical 
• Chemical 
• Semiconductor 
• Energy 
• Industrial Manufacturing 
From Communication Infrastructure to Mission-Critical Mobility
Through this partnership, TCE is expanding beyond communication infrastructure and positioning itself as a:
Mission-Critical Mobility Solutions Provider
By combining The Joy Factory’s HazLoc mobility solutions with enterprise mobility ecosystems, industrial 
companies can:
• Digitize inspection and maintenance workflows 
• Improve real-time field reporting 
• Enhance worker safety and compliance 
• Enable connected worker mobility in hazardous environments 
HazLoc Mobility Solution – HTA3021
A key solution in this partnership is  The Joy Factory HTA3021, an ATEX and IECEx certified flame-proof  
iPad enclosure designed for Zone 1 / Zone 21 hazardous environments.
The solution enables safe use of mobile devices in:
• Oil refineries & Offshore platforms 
• Petrochemical plants 
• Hazardous industrial sites 
It supports:
• Digital inspections 
• Maintenance workflows & Real-time reporting 
• Safety documentation 
• Connected worker applications

About The Joy Factory

hazardous environments.
