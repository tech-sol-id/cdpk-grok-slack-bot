Minimum Quantity: the minimum amount of Goods to be purchased for each Year as stated in Clause 5, or such other quantities as may be agreed in writing between the Parties in relation to each Year.

Order: an order for the Goods to be submitted by the Distributor to the Supplier during the Contract Period in accordance with this Agreement.

Territories: territories as stated in Clause 1.

Year: the period of Sixteen (16) month(s) from the Commencement Date.

A reference to a person includes a natural person, or a corporate or unincorporated body (whether or not having a separate legal personality).

APPOINTMENT OF DISTRIBUTOR

The Supplier appoints the Distributor as its distributor to import, market and distribute the Goods in the Territories during the Contract Period.

The Contract Period shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall continue for such period as stated in Clause 1 (or such other period as agreed in writing between the Distributor and the Supplier) unless terminated earlier:

as provided by the terms of this Agreement; or

by either Party giving to the other prior written notice of not less than the period as stated in Clause 1.

The Distributor will not:

sell, distribute or manufacture any goods which compete with the Goods during the Contract Period; or

sell, distribute or otherwise make available the Goods to distributors, resellers or end-users outside the Territories.

The Distributor will not:

represent itself as an agent of the Manufacturer for any purpose;

give any warranty or make any representation or any commitment on behalf of the Manufacturer and Supplier; or

incur any liability for or on behalf of the Manufacturer and Supplier.

SUPPLY OF GOODS

The Distributor will purchase the Goods for its own account for resale.

The Distributor will forecast the quantity of Goods to be ordered for each quarter and inform the Supplier in writing not less than two (2) week(s) prior to the commencement of such quarter.

The Distributor shall place Orders on a quarterly basis. Orders shall be made in writing in such form as designated by the Supplier and contain such information as from time to time required by the Supplier.

Each Order is a separate offer by the Distributor to purchase Goods on and subject to the terms of this Agreement. The Supplier may accept or decline Orders at its discretion provided that the Supplier must use all reasonable endeavors to meet Orders to the extent of the forecasted amount for the relevant period.

MINIMUM PURCHASE OBLIGATION

The Distributor will order at least the Minimum Quantity of the Goods for each Year during the Contract Period. Details of the Minimum Quantity are set out as follows:

Product Items:

Antenna (MA Series) with minimum of 100 units

ORU & OMU with a total of minimum of 20 units 

If the Distributor purchases less than the Minimum Quantity for any Year, the Distributor may carry forward any excess purchase over the Minimum Quantity made in the previous Year to make up the difference between the actual quantity purchased and the Minimum Quantity.

Upon the Distributor's failure to meet the Minimum Quantity for any Year (after carrying forward purchases from the previous Year), the Manufacturer and Supplier may, by giving notice in writing to the Distributor, withdraw of the Distributor right for the sale and distribution of the Goods in the Territories, and the relevant terms of this Agreement shall be deemed to be amended accordingly with effect from the date of the notice.

DELIVERY

The Goods will be delivered to the Delivery Location according to such delivery dates or schedule as agreed by the Distributor. Delivery dates are estimation only and the time of delivery is not of the essence. Upon giving reasonable notice to the Distributor, the Manufacturer and Supplier may deliver the Goods prior to the Delivery Date.

Delivery is completed on the completion of unloading of the Goods at the Delivery Location.

The Manufacturer and Supplier will not be liable for any delay or failure in delivery of the Goods if the delay or failure is caused by the Distributor's failure to provide accurate delivery instructions.

DISTRIBUTOR'S UNDERTAKING

The Distributor undertakes that at all times during the Contract Period it will:

use its best endeavors to promote the distribution and sale of the Goods in the Territories;

regularly report to the Supplier on the marketing and sales of the Goods;

keep proper books of account and records of all transactions and proceedings relating to the Goods with sufficient clarity, and allow the Supplier access to such accounts and records upon giving reasonable notice;

provide after-sale repair and maintenance services in respect of the Goods;

keep stocks of the Goods in appropriate conditions and adopt reasonable security measures in respect thereof; and

allow representatives or agents of the Manufacturer and Supplier access to the premises of the Distributor for inspection upon giving reasonable notice and at reasonable hours.

MANUFACTURER AND SUPPLIER'S UNDERTAKING

The Manufacturer and Supplier undertakes that at all times during the Contract Period it will:

use all reasonable endeavors to supply Goods to the Distributor for resale in the Territories to the extent of the Distributor's forecasted amounts;

supply any spare parts for the Goods requested by the Distributor, subject to availability;

provide reasonable assistance in the promotion of the Goods in the Territories upon request of the Distributor; and

provide sufficient training for the representatives of the Distributor in relation to the installation, use, sale, maintenance and repair (where applicable) of the Goods.

TITLE AND RISK

Risk in the Goods will pass to the Distributor upon unloading the Goods at the Delivery Location.

Title to the Goods will not pass to the Distributor until the earlier of:

full settlement of the Purchase Price; or

resale of the Goods in which case Clause 10.3 will apply.

Subject to Clause 10.4, the Distributor may resell or use (in its ordinary course of business) the Goods before the Purchase Price has been settled in full, in which case:

the Distributor resells the Goods as principal and not as an agent of the Supplier; and

title to the Goods will pass from the Supplier to the Distributor immediately before the time at which the resale occurs.

Where any of the events under Clause 16.1(d) to 16.1(h) occurs to the Distributor:

the Distributor's right to resell or use the Goods under Clause 10.3 will cease immediately; and

the Supplier may at any time require the Distributor to return all Goods in its possession that have not been resold, or where the Distributor fails to do so promptly, enter into the Distributor's premises or of any third party to retrieve the Goods to be returned.

Until title to the Goods passes to the Distributor, the Distributor undertakes:

to store the Goods separately from the Distributor's other goods such that the Goods remain readily identifiable as the Supplier's property;

TERMINATION

Without prejudice to any accrued rights or remedies available to it, either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by giving written notice to the other Party if at any time a Party:

commits any serious or repeated breach of any of the provisions of this Agreement;

(in the case of the Distributor) fails to meet the Minimum Quantity for any Year (after carrying forward purchases from the previous Year), both Parties should evaluate prior to sending their written notice as stated above;

(in the case of the Distributor) fails to pay the Purchase Price when they fall due for more than one (1) month(s);

suspends or ceases to carry on all or a substantial part of its business;

suspends or is unable to pay its debts when they fall due;

is (where the Party is an individual) declared bankrupt or makes any arrangement with or for the benefit of his creditors;

is (where the Party is an individual) incapable of performing his obligations under this Agreement (by reason of illness or incapacity or otherwise) for an extended period; or

is (where the Party is an entity) a petition is filed, a notice is given, a resolution is passed, or an order is made, for or in connection with the winding up of the Party (other than for the sole purpose of a scheme for a solvent amalgamation or solvent restructuring).

Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the Parties that have accrued up to the date of termination or expiry, including the right to claim damages in respect of any breach of the Agreement which existed at or before the date of termination or expiry.

On termination the Supplier will have the option to buy back all or part of the stocks of the Goods at the same price at which the Distributor purchased them. Such option must be exercised within one (undefined) month(s) from the effective date of termination by giving notice in writing to the Distributor, specifying the quantity of Goods the Supplier intends to buy back. Within two (2) week(s) from such notice, the Distributor must deliver the relevant quantity of Goods to the Supplier.

Where the Manufacturer and Supplier chooses not to exercise its option to buy back all or any of the Goods or fails to exercise its right under Clause 16.3 within the specified period, the Distributor shall be free to sell or distribute any remaining stocks of the Goods under its control, or dispose of the remaining stocks as the Manufacturer and Supplier directs.

FORCE MAJEURE

Neither Party shall be in breach of this Agreement or be liable for delay in performing, or failure to perform, any of its obligations under this Agreement if such delay or failure result from events, circumstances or causes beyond its reasonable control. In such circumstances, the time for performance shall be extended by a period equivalent to the period during which performance of the obligation has been delayed or failed to be performed.

A force majeure event includes: an act of God, fire, flood, typhoon, storm, war, riot, civil unrest, act of terrorism, strike, industrial dispute, major economic recession, outbreak of epidemic or pandemic illness, or failure of utility service or transportation.

If the period of delay or non-performance continues for twelve (12) month(s), the Party not affected may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice of one (1) month(s) to the affected Party (or whether both Parties are affected by the force majeure event, either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice of one (1) month(s) to the other Party).
