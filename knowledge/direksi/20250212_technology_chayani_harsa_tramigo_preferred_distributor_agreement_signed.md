2. DEFINITIONS 
When used in this Preferred Distributor and Stockist Agreement (“Agreement”), the following terms shall 
have the meanings indicated. The meanings shall be applied whether the singular or the plural form of 
the term is used: 
“Activation Key” means the activation of a Solution for 1-year usage through Tramigo reseller platform. 
“Affiliate” means any company controlled by, controlling, or under common control with a party hereto. 
“Territory” means the following geographic area or areas: Indonesia 
“SIM” shall mean the Tramigo IoT SIM/eSIM identified by a unique “ICCID” number and Tramigo Device 
with embedded eSIM identified by a unique “IMEI” number. 
“Services” shall mean the data connectivity enabled by the SIM, as well as the Tramigo proprietary 
software. 
“Solution(s)” shall mean the bundle of Tramigo Devices (hardware) and Services as per Appendix 1. 
3. APPOINTMENT OF DISTRIBUTOR 
3.1 Supplier hereby appoints Distributor as Supplier’s non-exclusive Preferred Distributor of Tramigo 
Solutions in the Territory, for the  next 24 months as of the signing date as long as the Distributor complies 
with clause 4 and adheres to the General Terms & Conditions regarding provided Services which forms a 
part of this agreement: https://tramigo.com/general-terms-and-conditions/ 
3.2 Distributor is an independent contractor and not a legal representative or agent of Supplier for any 
purpose and shall have no right or authority (except as expressly provided in this Agreement) to incur, 
assume or create in writing or otherwise, any warranty on the part of Supplier. 
3.3 Distributor is to uphold the reputation of Supplier in all its actions and business practises. 
 
4. TERMS OF PURCHASE AND SALE OF SOLUTIONS 
4.1 Distributor shall purchase the Solutions only from Supplier or its Affiliates.  
4.2 All orders shall be paid by cash in advance unless otherwise agreed.  
4.3 All Solutions purchased shall be shipped as EXW Jakarta (Incoterms 2023). 
4.4 Distributor shall supply a rolling three quarters forecast starting Q2 for excess volumes above the below 
minimum volumes. 
Premium models 
For Tramigo’s MCPTT-1 with 4-6 weeks lead time delivery and MCPTT-2 Solutions with 8 weeks lead time 
delivery, the Distributor is appointed as the Exclusive Stockist against following terms:  
 Initial Order of minimum 1’000 pcs of MCPTT-1 and 1’000 pcs of MCPTT-2 ordered and paid 50% 
before 20th February 2025 and remaining 50% before 15th March 2025.  
 As of Q2 2025, minimum quarterly (every 3 months) performance commitment of 2’000 units per 
Solution to be eligible as Exclusive Stockist Distributor. 50% of the minimum quarterly order to be 
paid before the start of the quarter (e.g. for Q2 50% to be paid latest 31st of March) and the remaining 
50% before confirmed delivery date. Partial deliveries allowed subject to mutual agreement.  
 In case minimum quarterly orders are not paid, model exclusivity changes to non-exclusive status. No 
other impact. 
 If Distributor has unsold stocks of more than 3’000 unsold products of a specific exclusivity product, 
new orders are not required for the next quarter, and exclusivity shall remain. 
 These premium market Solutions are to serve B2B and B2G projects, and Tramigo will refer its 
customers to the Distributor.

5 MARKETING AND SALES SUPPORT 
5.1 Supplier shall offer consultation and training for the personnel of the Distributor. 
5.2 Tramigo lists the Preferred Distributor on its internet page and offers marketing support primarily 
focusing on social media, PPC (Pay-Per-Click) and other forms of internet marketing. Distributor may 
suggest other local marketing methods and Tramigo may participate on case-by-case basis. 
 
6 APPLICABLE LAW AND SETTLEMENT OF DISPUTES 
The parties agree that this Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of 
Indonesia. Parties aim to solve any arising dispute amicably. 
Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, or the breach, termination or 
validity thereof shall be finally settled by arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the BANI 
Arbitration Center. The arbitration shall take place in Jakarta, and the language of the pleadings shall be 
English. The BANI Arbitration Center shall appoint the one and only arbitrator.
