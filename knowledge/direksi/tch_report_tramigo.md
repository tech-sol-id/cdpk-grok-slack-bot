Current Business Review (2/4)
Feedback from end-users and sales team
end-user
Tramigo is considered a modern communication solution based on Push-to-Talk over Cellular (PoC) which has the advantage of a multi-network eSIM, capable of automatically switching to the best network (Telkomsel, XL, Indosat) without the need for a SIM replacement.
Voice delay (latency) that is still felt under certain network conditions, even though the product is known as "Mission Critical"
License price perceived as high for entry-level market
sales team
The license price is too expensive for end users.
The MCPTT 2 is difficult to sell in the field even though it has NFC features, losing out to the price of the Hytera PNC 380.
In the field, many competitors sell lifetime licenses.
Challenge faced in converting leads
Lack of TKDN certification – limiting access to government projects that require TKDN
Annual subscription-based licensing vs. perpetual licensing from other competitors
Limited market awareness of the PoC value proposition
ISO Certification is a must for certain projects

Distributor’s current sales structure and coverage

We then shifted our strategy to build a partnership-based sales ecosystem — starting from project networks, and expanding through resellers and partners to broaden market coverage.
