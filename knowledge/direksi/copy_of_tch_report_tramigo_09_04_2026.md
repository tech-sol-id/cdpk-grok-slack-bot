Current Business Review (2/6)
Feedback from end-users and sales team
end-user
There is a misperception of product positioning, where Tramigo is often compared to entry-level brands (e.g., Osaka, Kirisun) instead of being recognized as a premium PoC solution.
prefer a lifetime licensing model that provides flexibility in managing their monthly usage or quotas.
Moreover, there is an expectation for a lower total cost of ownership, especially among entry-level or price-sensitive segments.
Occasional concerns regarding latency under unstable network conditions, which can impact the perception of “mission-critical” reliability.
sales team
Sales frequently face challenges in educating the market on the value differentiation vs lower-tier competitors.
The subscription-based licensing model is harder to justify (just like WavePTX Motorola) compared to competitors offering one-time (lifetime) licenses (e.g. POCStar and Hytalk).
Price sensitivity remains a key barrier, especially when competing with brands positioned in the low-to-mid segment. (MCPTT 1 & MCPTT 2)
Product features (e.g., NFC, multi-network eSIM) are not always fully appreciated, making value-based selling more difficult.
Mostly customers already have corporate provider, they would like to switch off the e-sim.

Current Business Review (3/6)
Challenges faced in converting leads
Product positioning gap: The market still perceives PoC devices as commodities rather than differentiated solutions.
Mismatch in licensing expectations: The Market prefers a lifetime + flexible usage model vs. the current subscription scheme.
Low awareness of PoC advantages, especially compared to conventional radio systems.
Compliance requirements (e.g., ISO certification) remain a barrier for specific tenders/projects. All government related project is a MUST HAVE as their standard bidding. (Hytera Janz PNC 380 : TKDN 31,44%)
